Access difficulty — discuss feasibility first

Data access warning: the required datasets may be difficult or costly to obtain. Verify availability, coverage and permitted use with your supervisor before committing. This topic is not currently ready for assignment.

A synthetic core does not require external data; empirical and hybrid projects require suitable coverage and permission. A data status is not a guarantee of availability or a licence. Do not purchase or substitute datasets without discussing scope and cost.

Discuss feasibility and supervision

Research proposals for exploration, not confirmed assignments. Agree scope, data access and supervision before starting. Complete student research drafts are available for consultation; assignment still requires confirmation.

Prepare the research environment

Open a complete draft workspace to read the proposal, methods, literature, data guide and plan. Download the starter notebook or original workspace ZIP. The Python environment is available for independent exploration; no solved thesis or empirical dataset is supplied.
