A04.03.01 · Asset Pricing · Macro-finance & policy

Monetary Policy Surprises and the Stock Market

How strongly do US equity prices respond to unexpected shifts in the expected policy-rate path around FOMC communications, and does the response differ between statements and press conferences? Assess conditional associations without automatically assigning a causal structural policy shock interpretation.

Reading focus: Window-specific equity response to a rates-only FOMC path surprise, contrasting statements and conferences.

Window and event matching, units and sign must be pinned to the exact provider release. The surprise can mix policy and central-bank information; sorting using equity sign and then regressing that equity outcome on the sorted shock is mechanical. Treat statement and conference observations of one meeting as dependent. HC3 or meeting-cluster SE alone is not a small-sample certificate.
