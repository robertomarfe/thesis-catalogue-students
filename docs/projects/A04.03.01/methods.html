A04.03.01 · Asset Pricing · Macro-finance & policy

Monetary Policy Surprises and the Stock Market

Research question

How strongly do US equity prices respond to unexpected shifts in the expected policy-rate path around FOMC communications, and does the response differ between statements and press conferences? Assess conditional associations without automatically assigning a causal structural policy shock interpretation.

Conditional data access · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Conditional data access. USMPD event and surprise workbooks; matched equity windows, units and rights.

Focused methods / A04.03.01

1. Event and unit definitions

A valid key is (meeting,window,start UTC,end UTC)(\text{meeting},\text{window},\text{start UTC},\text{end UTC}). Check uniqueness and the documented 30- or 70-minute length. Preserve the raw rate and equity units before normalizing. A change of 0.0025 in a decimal rate equals 25 basis points; an equity decimal return of 0.01 equals 1 percent.

For a window-specific regression ye=α+βse+uey_e=\alpha+\beta s_e+u_e, the coefficient has units of decimal equity return per basis point. Report 100×25β̂100\times25\hat\beta as the estimated percent equity response to a 25-bp surprise. Scale confidence limits in the same way.

2. Paired meeting design

For meetings containing both windows define De=1D_e=1 for a conference and zero for a statement. Estimate

ye=α+βse+ηDe+δDese+ue.y_e=\alpha+\beta s_e+\eta D_e+\delta D_e s_e+u_e.

The statement slope is β\beta, the conference slope is β+δ\beta+\delta. Cluster by meeting date; this accounts for dependence between the two observations of the same meeting. A regression on every observed statement is a different sample from a matched-pair regression. Report both sample definitions.

3. Identification and reporting

Use a surprise constructed without the same equity return being explained. A surprise may combine policy and central-bank information, even in a short window. Conditioning on the sign of the equity response and then estimating that response is mechanically endogenous. Treat rate-only regressions as conditional associations unless a separately justified structural identification strategy is available.
