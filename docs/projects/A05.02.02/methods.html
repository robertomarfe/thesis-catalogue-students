Principal Components and the Dynamics of Government Bond Yields

Research question

How stable are the dimensionality and loading shapes of government yield curves across the US and euro area and across time?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Statistical object

Use decimal yields or bp consistently; rescaling all maturities together leaves covariance eigenvectors unchanged. Correlation PCA standardizes each maturity, so reconstruction must reverse the training scaling. Persistent yield levels and innovations are different covariance objects.

Stability

For orthonormal loading matrices V,W∈ℝm×kV,W\in\mathbb R^{m\times k}, singular values of V⊤WV^\top W equal cosines of principal angles. These are invariant to signs and rotations within the subspace. Report eigenvalue gaps and held-out error by maturity. Align curves on actual common dates and quote type; US off-the-run and euro AAA issuer universes are not identical risk-free instruments.

Research checks

Separate reconstruction from forecasting. Near tied eigenvalues, use subspace comparisons rather than interpreting a particular eigenvector as an economic structural shock.
