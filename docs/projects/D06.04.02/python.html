Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Fixing currency conversion removes direct exchange-rate exposure at settlement but changes the pricing-measure equity drift. The quanto correction depends on risk-neutral equity–FX covariance. The study separates fixed, random and spot conversion, and evaluates both price and equity-delta exposure.

Research question: How do FX volatility and equity-FX correlation affect quanto prices and deltas relative to ordinary foreign options converted at spot?

Student-owned research

Check the discounted domestic stock martingale and currency units. Hold contractual conversion fixed for delta. Validate random conversion by numeraire invariance; do not use physical correlation as risk-neutral calibration.

  1. Derive drift and pricing identity, specify currency units.

  2. Verify domestic martingale for XS and random-conversion benchmark.

  3. Check sigma_X=0/rho=0 and deterministic FX carry conventions.

  4. Compute quanto price, delta and correlation sensitivity across grid with MC confidence intervals.

  5. Stress reversed quote with correct numeraire/drift transformation; distinguish fixed conversion from FX-hedged foreign position. For each FX-volatility and correlation pair, compute analytic, central-bump and pathwise or independent Monte Carlo spot delta with FX spot and contractual conversion fixed. Match the spot-converted foreign option in domestic units.

Minimum viable thesis: Analytic and Monte Carlo quanto call prices and spot deltas for two FX volatilities and three equity–FX correlations at one maturity; validate the analytic spot delta by a central bump and a pathwise or independently simulated estimate, holding the fixed conversion rate and FX spot fixed. Include the random-conversion control and the matched spot-converted foreign option price and delta.
