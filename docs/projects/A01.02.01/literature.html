A01.02.01 · Asset Pricing · Cross-sectional pricing & SDF

Three Factors or Five? Comparing Fama-French Asset Pricing Models

Research question

Does the Fama-French five-factor model improve pricing of US industry and characteristic portfolios relative to the three-factor model, and are the gains stable across samples? Do the additional factors have explanatory power conditional on common market, size and value factors?

Reading guide | A01.02.01

Read in this order alongside the Methods Note; publisher metadata or source description checked 2026-11-25, not all full texts reviewed. Obtain lawful access through the library.

1. Fama and French (1993), Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and Bonds, JFE 33, 3–56

Read the construction and time-series pricing-error tests. The original paper also studies bond factors; our stock-factor comparison is not a full replication.

2. Fama and French (2015), A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model, JFE 116, 1–22

Read profitability, investment and model comparison; interpret HML redundancy conditionally rather than as a universal fact.

3. Gibbons, Ross and Shanken (1989), A Test of the Efficiency of a Given Portfolio, Econometrica 57, 1121–1152

Derive the joint intercept restriction and finite-sample statistic; compare its assumptions with HAC and block-bootstrap diagnostics.
