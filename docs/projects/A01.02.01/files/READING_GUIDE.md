### 1. [Fama and French (1993), Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and Bonds, JFE 33, 3–56](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-405X(93)90023-5)
Read the construction and time-series pricing-error tests. The original paper also studies bond factors; our stock-factor comparison is not a full replication.

### 2. [Fama and French (2015), A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model, JFE 116, 1–22](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2014.10.010)
Read profitability, investment and model comparison; interpret HML redundancy conditionally rather than as a universal fact.

### 3. [Gibbons, Ross and Shanken (1989), A Test of the Efficiency of a Given Portfolio, Econometrica 57, 1121–1152](https://doi.org/10.2307/1913625)
Derive the joint intercept restriction and finite-sample statistic; compare its assumptions with HAC and block-bootstrap diagnostics.
