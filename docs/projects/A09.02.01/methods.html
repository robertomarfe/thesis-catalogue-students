The Forward Premium Puzzle and Currency Carry

Research question

Does the observed forward premium forecast subsequent spot appreciation as UIP implies, and how does this bilateral evidence relate to published currency carry returns?

Conditional data access · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Conditional data access. Observed BoE forward series and matching G10 carry workbook; coverage and rights.

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Quote orientation and UIP

Use StS_t and FtF_t in USD per GBP with matched fixing and tenor. The forward premium is ft−stf_t-s_t in log units; expected log spot appreciation equals this premium under the usual log-linear UIP benchmark. The regression null is (α,β)=(0,1)(\alpha,\beta)=(0,1) under that benchmark; Jensen terms and currency risk premia qualify its interpretation. The long-GBP forward’s USD payoff per GBP is St+1−FtS_{t+1}-F_t; the log difference st+1−fts_{t+1}-f_t is not a funded simple return. Reversing both quotes reverses these log differences.

Timing and distinct evidence

Verify the original BoE series and actual termination date locally; the inherited design requests a forward sample ending in 2021 and the following spot endpoint. Month-end observations approximate settlement and are not executable trades. Exclude missing calendar pairs instead of compressing time. Published G10 gross carry is a separate cross-sectional payoff; document portfolio sign and normalization, and use net only as documented sensitivity. Use common monthly dates, dependence-aware uncertainty and a distinct, explicitly approved fallback if the actual quotes cannot be obtained.

Research checks

Before estimation verify outright versus points, quote orientation, fixing and outcome dates; audit rights and actual coverage. Do not splice observed and synthetic forwards or infer G10 carry from a bilateral regression.
