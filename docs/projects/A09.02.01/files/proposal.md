## Motivation

The forward premium puzzle concerns the relationship between observed bilateral forward prices and subsequent exchange-rate changes. A published cross-sectional carry factor is a different object: it combines currencies and portfolio formation rules. Keep these two sources of evidence separate.

## Research question

Does the observed forward premium forecast subsequent spot appreciation as UIP implies, and how does this bilateral evidence relate to published currency carry returns?

## Economic mechanism

With $s_t=\log S_t$ and $f_t=\log F_t$, estimate $s_{t+1}-s_t=\alpha+\beta(f_t-s_t)+\epsilon_{t+1}$.

## Research design

Subject to the conditional source gate, obtain the prescribed BoE GBP/USD spot and one-month outright forward, verify USD/GBP orientation and comparable fixings, and pair each forward with its next settlement proxy. Test the UIP slope and separately characterize documented G10 carry returns. No synthetic replacement without approval.

Before estimation verify outright versus points, quote orientation, fixing and outcome dates; audit rights and actual coverage. Do not splice observed and synthetic forwards or infer G10 carry from a bilateral regression.

## Scope and feasibility

Do not extend actual-forward sample using synthetic forwards silently. Historical BoE midquotes are not executable returns, exact value dates may differ from month-end. Published baskets do not disclose underlying individual quotes; do not reconstruct them from portfolio returns. Log approximations and Jensen terms matter, inference low power for persistent premia.
