Research plan | A11.04.02

Study design and independent work

Preserve 5,000 common samples at the specified 80/200 horizons, positivity and conditional moments, and prespecified noisy proxies. Compare paired inference, and distinguish a failure of the constant kernel from an identification of market inefficiency.

Required tasks

  1. Verify positivity, exact state Euler moments and equivalent return distributions.

  2. Simulate and compare predictive coefficient/R2 distributions under identical returns.

  3. Test conditional Euler restrictions for true m and constant m0 using common samples and instruments.

  4. Vary persistence, sample size and SDF measurement noise; compare power and size.

  5. Derive what observing an independent consumption or cross-asset restriction could add; state maintained assumptions.

Required exhibits

Euler moment table, simulation distributions, rejection rates and observational equivalence diagram.

Robustness

Do not label constant-SDF failure as proof of market inefficiency; joint hypothesis requires a maintained pricing model. The behavioral interpretation is not a complete equilibrium. Report finite-sample inference and alternative admissible SDFs.

Minimum viable thesis

Exact two-state construction, shared simulated returns, predictive regressions and Euler tests.

Optional extension

Add a second traded payoff and show how spanning and independent SDF restrictions reduce admissible explanations.

Prerequisites

Conditional expectation, Markov chains, asset pricing, regression.

Thesis outline

  1. Efficiency and joint hypothesis;

  2. Two-state construction;

  3. Simulation;

  4. Discriminating restrictions;

  5. Limits.

Milestones

W1-3: exact model; W4-6: simulate and verify; W7-9: predictive tests; W10-12: Euler power; W13-16: writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
