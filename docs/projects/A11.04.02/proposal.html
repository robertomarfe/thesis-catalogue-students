Asset pricing · Cross-sectional pricing and SDF · Level 2/3

Data: Synthetic core; no mandatory external data. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Predictability can coexist with a positive stochastic discount factor that prices all modeled payoffs. A misspecified constant kernel can fail on the same return history. Identical predictive moments thus cannot distinguish a risk-premium mechanism from a reduced-form distortion without auxiliary economic restrictions.

Research question

Can identical predictive return moments arise under a valid time-varying-risk-premium SDF and a behavioral price-distortion interpretation, and what extra restrictions would distinguish them?

Economic mechanism

For state ztz_t, Rt+1=Rf+μ(zt)+sϵt+1R_{t+1}=R_f+\mu(z_t)+s\epsilon_{t+1} with symmetric ϵ∈{−1,1}\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}; evaluate conditional pricing restrictions separately from the predictive regression.

Research design

Use the frozen two-state economy and common return draws for both interpretations, including the two prescribed sample lengths and auxiliary consumption proxies. Derive the exact construction, then compare conditional Euler diagnostics and sampling uncertainty on shared paths.

Preserve 5,000 common samples at the specified 80/200 horizons, positivity and conditional moments, and prespecified noisy proxies. Compare paired inference, and distinguish a failure of the constant kernel from an identification of market inefficiency.

Required exhibits

Euler moment table, simulation distributions, rejection rates and observational equivalence diagram.

Minimum viable thesis

Exact two-state construction, shared simulated returns, predictive regressions and Euler tests.

Scope and feasibility

Do not label constant-SDF failure as proof of market inefficiency; joint hypothesis requires a maintained pricing model. The behavioral interpretation is not a complete equilibrium. Report finite-sample inference and alternative admissible SDFs.

Prerequisites: Conditional expectation, Markov chains, asset pricing, regression.

Optional extension: Add a second traded payoff and show how spanning and independent SDF restrictions reduce admissible explanations.

Milestones

W1-3: exact model; W4-6: simulate and verify; W7-9: predictive tests; W10-12: Euler power; W13-16: writing.
