The Impact of Non-Normal Returns on European Option Pricing

Research question

How do risk-neutral skewness and excess kurtosis change European option prices and implied volatility smiles when forward and log variance are matched?

A forward-normalized terminal law

Let F=𝔼ℚ[ST]F=\mathbb E^{\mathbb Q}[S_T], D=e−rTD=e^{-rT} and X=log(ST/F)X=\log(S_T/F). Begin with YY distributed as a finite Gaussian mixture with weights pjp_j, component means μj\mu_j and variances sj2s_j^2. The unconditional mean is μ‾=∑jpjμj\bar\mu=\sum_jp_j\mu_j and variance is

vY=∑jpj[sj2+(μj−μ‾)2].v_Y=\sum_jp_j[s_j^2+(\mu_j-\bar\mu)^2].
Center and rescale to the target log variance. For the resulting variable ZZ, set X=Z−log𝔼[eZ]X=Z-\log\mathbb E[e^Z]. This common shift preserves all central moments and ensures 𝔼[eX]=1\mathbb E[e^X]=1. It changes the log mean and need not match the arithmetic variance of STS_T.

Tail information and prices

If dj=μj−μ‾d_j=\mu_j-\bar\mu, the third and fourth central moments of the initial mixture are

∑jpj(dj3+3djsj2),∑jpj(dj4+6dj2sj2+3sj4).\sum_jp_j(d_j^3+3d_js_j^2),\quad \sum_jp_j(d_j^4+6d_j^2s_j^2+3s_j^4).
Divide by vY3/2v_Y^{3/2} and vY2v_Y^2 respectively, subtracting 3 for excess kurtosis. Conditional on a component of the transformed mixture, the stock is lognormal; its forward is Femj+wj/2F e^{m_j+w_j/2} for conditional log mean mjm_j and variance wjw_j. Integrate the discounted call payoff over each component and then aggregate by its probability. Compare exact calculations with independent simulation, and invert only prices strictly within Black bounds. Equal skewness and kurtosis do not force equality of option prices at all strikes. This terminal exercise does not impose cross-maturity consistency.

Research checks

Match log variance and forward before reporting skew or kurtosis. Include a symmetric variance mixture, an asymmetric location mixture and its mirror. Use the same strike coordinates and display the actual moments alongside the smile; the arithmetic variance match is a robustness exercise.
