Reading focus: Cross-sectional inflation beta and descriptive mean-beta relation, distinct from A04.02.02 purchasing-power hedging.

Transforming the mean annualized SPF forecast is not averaging individual quarterly rates. Real excess returns require deflating both legs by the same realized inflation. Generated inflation and market betas share common calendar errors; a cross-section of related portfolios is not an independent sample or a structural price-of-risk estimate.
