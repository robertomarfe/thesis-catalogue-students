Asian Option Pricing and the Value of Averaging

Research question

How do the averaging window, monitoring frequency and volatility affect arithmetic and geometric Asian call prices and their hedge sensitivities relative to a European option?

Geometric and arithmetic averages

For exact GBM, logStj=logS0+(r−q−σ2/2)tj+σWtj\log S_{t_j}=\log S_0+(r-q-\sigma^2/2)t_j+\sigma W_{t_j}. Hence logG\log G has mean μG=logS0+(r−q−σ2/2)t‾\mu_G=\log S_0+(r-q-\sigma^2/2)\bar t and variance sG2=σ2m−2∑i,jmin(ti,tj)s_G^2=\sigma^2m^{-2}\sum_{i,j}\min(t_i,t_j). Its mean level is eμG+sG2/2e^{\mu_G+s_G^2/2}. Derive a lognormal call expectation and discount to payment TT. The arithmetic average is not generally lognormal; use the geometric call as a control with an independent pilot coefficient.

Schedule and exposure

One fixing at TT reduces to a European claim, and A≥GA\ge G implies ordering for matched Asian calls. Nonzero carry changes expected average levels when dates change. Estimate delta and vega at fixed contractual dates and strike using common normals and bump convergence. All fixings are future at initiation; realized fixings would change the state and sensitivity convention.

Research checks

Change frequency and window separately. Use union-grid draws where practical because grids are not nested. Preserve payment time; do not call a finite fixing grid a continuous average.
