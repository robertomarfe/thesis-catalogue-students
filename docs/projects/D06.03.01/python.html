D06.03.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Principal protection uses part of issue proceeds to finance a promised maturity floor, leaving a residual budget for equity exposure. Low yields, fees and issuer credit therefore affect attainable participation. The project studies the financing constraint and distinguishes promised from reliable protection.

Research question: How do yield level, volatility, fees and issuer credit affect attainable equity participation and the economic value of principal protection?

Student-owned research

Reconcile components and proceeds for feasible scenarios; retain infeasible ones. Separate Q prices and P losses. The optional GSW curve is sovereign, not issuer funding or collateral.

  1. Derive replication and feasibility condition; verify call price, par cost and payoff by scenarios.

  2. Plot alpha versus rates, maturity, implied vol and fees, including infeasible cells.

  3. Compare sensitivities of note value and participation holding contract versus redesigning at issue.

  4. Simulate terminal nominal and real (fixed inflation scenario) returns for note, stock and bond, include default loss.

  5. Vary hazard and recovery convention in clearly separate scenarios; compare independent default to optional adverse state dependence.

Minimum viable thesis: Default-free replication, feasible budget grid and unsecured zero-recovery scenario; no observed structured-note data required. Distinct from D06.03.02’s autocallable complexity.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
