D04.04.01 · Derivatives · Hedging & volatility derivatives

Deep Hedging with Transaction Costs

Can a small neural hedging policy improve the net loss distribution of a short option relative to delta hedging and tuned no-trade bands at the same cost and risk objective?

Synthetic core · Level 3/3 · Research draft

Synthetic core. No external dataset required for the core design; optional extensions may require data.

Theory & methods

Open methods.pdfOpen methods.md

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Objective and information

Map (log(Si/K),T−ti,hi−1)(\log(S_i/K),T-t_i,h_{i-1}) to bounded shares. Use one discounted seller-loss ledger: payoff minus clean premium minus stock gains plus proportional costs. Entropic risk is translation equivariant and η\eta has inverse-currency units. Use stable log-sum-exp for empirical evaluation. Future returns are not policy inputs.

Fair comparison

Use Q for the common premium and P for simulated outcomes; fix decision dates, costs and constraints across neural, delta and band policies. Separate training, validation and test paths before fitting. Retain optimization failures and report training variability rather than selecting a favorable test seed.

Research checks

The student develops the network, objective, ledger and complete comparison. Report tail loss, turnover, risk objective and stability; in-sample improvement alone is insufficient.
