Reading guide

  1. Liu, Y., and A. Tsyvinski (2021), Review of Financial Studies 34, 2689–2727, DOI. Read the treatment of conventional and cryptocurrency-specific risks. Their investigation goes beyond our single-asset exposure/stability exercise; do not assume their findings hold for this sample.
  2. Fama, E. F., and K. R. French (2015), Journal of Financial Economics 116, 1–22, DOI. Understand the traded factor definitions and distinguish stock cross-sectional pricing tests from a time-series exposure regression for Bitcoin. Use the official Data Library for current conventions.
  3. Newey, W. K., and K. D. West (1987), Econometrica 55, 703–708, DOI, working paper. Work through Bartlett weights, sandwich covariance and regular spacing. HAC is asymptotic; it does not remove nonstationarity, measurement error or small sample uncertainty.

Metadata and stated abstract-level contributions checked 25 November 2026. Full text and supplementary replication inputs not audited. Prepare a one-page note distinguishing factor exposure, diversification and asset pricing.
