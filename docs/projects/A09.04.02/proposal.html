Data: Empirical design blocked pending authorized access and coverage; not assignable on this design. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Bitcoin trades through weekends and around the clock, unlike the equity portfolios used to construct conventional factors. Apparent changes in market exposure may depend on asynchronous month-end measurement as well as economic integration. Authorized and correctly timed input data are essential.

Research question

How stable is Bitcoin’s exposure to conventional equity factors and lagged stress conditions, and how much does time aggregation affect this inference?

Economic mechanism

For a common monthly calendar, rBTC,t=Pt/Pt−1−1r_{BTC,t}=P_t/P_{t-1}-1; compare rBTC,t−rf,t=α+β⊤ft+etr_{BTC,t}-r_{f,t}=\alpha+\beta^\top f_t+e_t.

Research design

Subject to written permission or an explicitly approved authorized alternative, obtain the specified Coinbase BTC USD series, FF3 and VIX. Construct exact calendar month-end returns including the prior endpoint, and a separately audited last-US-trading-day alternative. Compare full-sample and rolling factor exposures on common dates.

Blocked until permission, actual coverage and timestamp are validated. Retain the baseline 72-month and split 36-month thresholds from the input protocol; require exact prior endpoints and documented timing robustness. No unapproved substitute or raw redistribution.

Required exhibits

Aligned sample audit, rolling betas, split regressions, covariance/tail table and timing sensitivity.

Minimum viable thesis

Authorized BTC and FF3 common monthly sample, baseline and rolling beta, one timing robustness.

Scope and feasibility

Small sample, heavy tails, weekend trading and asynchronous timing; no extrapolation to other crypto assets. Access at no cost is not redistribution permission. No strategy profitability or causal claim.

Prerequisites: Return construction, factor models, regression, Python.

Optional extension: Authorized ETH series and common-date comparative analysis; no survivorship-free crypto universe claim.

Milestones

W1-3: rights and data; W4-6: aggregation/regression; W7-9: rolling and conditional beta; W10-12: diversification/robustness; W13-16: writing.
