Research plan | A09.04.02

Study design and independent work

Blocked until permission, actual coverage and timestamp are validated. Retain the baseline 72-month and split 36-month thresholds from the input protocol; require exact prior endpoints and documented timing robustness. No unapproved substitute or raw redistribution.

Required tasks

  1. Audit BTC timestamp, gaps and monthly aggregation; align RF and factor units.

  2. Estimate FF3 and market-only exposures with HAC and residual diagnostics.

  3. Compare rolling 36-month beta and predeclared split 2015-19/2020-25.

  4. Estimate lagged-stress interactions and fixed-weight BTC/equity covariance, tail loss and turnover-free illustrative returns.

  5. Repeat alternative timestamp and exclude extreme months one at a time; report intervals.

Required exhibits

Aligned sample audit, rolling betas, split regressions, covariance/tail table and timing sensitivity.

Robustness

Small sample, heavy tails, weekend trading and asynchronous timing; no extrapolation to other crypto assets. Access at no cost is not redistribution permission. No strategy profitability or causal claim.

Minimum viable thesis

Authorized BTC and FF3 common monthly sample, baseline and rolling beta, one timing robustness.

Optional extension

Authorized ETH series and common-date comparative analysis; no survivorship-free crypto universe claim.

Prerequisites

Return construction, factor models, regression, Python.

Thesis outline

  1. Crypto and equity risk;

  2. Factor models;

  3. Timing/data;

  4. Stability and diversification;

  5. Limits.

Milestones

W1-3: rights and data; W4-6: aggregation/regression; W7-9: rolling and conditional beta; W10-12: diversification/robustness; W13-16: writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
