Digital Assets and Traditional Asset Pricing Factors

How stable is Bitcoin's exposure to conventional equity factors and lagged stress conditions, and how much does time aggregation affect this inference?

Research overview

Motivation

Bitcoin trades through weekends and around the clock, unlike the equity portfolios used to construct conventional factors. Apparent changes in market exposure may depend on asynchronous month-end measurement as well as economic integration. Authorized and correctly timed input data are essential.

Minimum viable thesis

Authorized BTC and FF3 common monthly sample, baseline and rolling beta, one timing robustness.

Prerequisites

Return construction, factor models, regression, Python.

Methods

Regression & inference · Time-series forecasting · Factor models & SDF · Tail risk & distributions
