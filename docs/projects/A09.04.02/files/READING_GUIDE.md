# Reading guide

1. Liu, Y., and A. Tsyvinski (2021), Review of Financial Studies 34, 2689–2727, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa113). Read the treatment of conventional and cryptocurrency-specific risks. Their investigation goes beyond our single-asset exposure/stability exercise; do not assume their findings hold for this sample.
2. Fama, E. F., and K. R. French (2015), Journal of Financial Economics 116, 1–22, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2014.10.010). Understand the traded factor definitions and distinguish stock cross-sectional pricing tests from a time-series exposure regression for Bitcoin. Use the official [Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) for current conventions.
3. Newey, W. K., and K. D. West (1987), Econometrica 55, 703–708, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/1913610), [working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/t0055). Work through Bartlett weights, sandwich covariance and regular spacing. HAC is asymptotic; it does not remove nonstationarity, measurement error or small sample uncertainty.
