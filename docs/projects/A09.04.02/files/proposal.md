## Motivation

Bitcoin trades through weekends and around the clock, unlike the equity portfolios used to construct conventional factors. Apparent changes in market exposure may depend on asynchronous month-end measurement as well as economic integration. Authorized and correctly timed input data are essential.

## Research question

How stable is Bitcoin's exposure to conventional equity factors and lagged stress conditions, and how much does time aggregation affect this inference?

## Economic mechanism

For a common monthly calendar, $r_{BTC,t}=P_t/P_{t-1}-1$; compare $r_{BTC,t}-r_{f,t}=\alpha+\beta^\top f_t+e_t$.

## Research design

Subject to written permission or an explicitly approved authorized alternative, obtain the specified Coinbase BTC USD series, FF3 and VIX. Construct exact calendar month-end returns including the prior endpoint, and a separately audited last-US-trading-day alternative. Compare full-sample and rolling factor exposures on common dates.

Blocked until permission, actual coverage and timestamp are validated. Retain the baseline 72-month and split 36-month thresholds from the input protocol; require exact prior endpoints and documented timing robustness. No unapproved substitute or raw redistribution.
