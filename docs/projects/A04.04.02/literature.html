Reading focus: Lagged financial stress conditions stock-bond covariance and diversification of a fixed portfolio, not hedge exposures or dynamic allocation.

Observation week ending Friday is not release time; revised historical stress is not point-in-time. Regime threshold cannot be chosen using future returns; correlation can move while portfolio variance falls because marginal volatilities differ. Few crisis episodes make iid SE misleading.
