Current review draft; approval pending. Empirical core; actual rights, coverage and input validation pending.

Goal

Factor strategies can have different payoffs in rising and falling markets even when their unconditional market exposure is small. This project documents conditional payoffs and their stability; it does not attempt to infer a separate cross-sectional price of downside risk.

Research question: Which equity factors have asymmetric market exposures and payoff distributions across down and up markets, and how stable is the asymmetry?

Student-owned research

Prespecify the sign cutoff and rolling windows. Report all factors, state counts and crisis sensitivity; distinguish conditional descriptive analysis from a trading signal or a pricing test.

  1. Describe factor means, volatility, lower partial moments and payoff distributions in up/down states.

  2. Estimate separate and interaction regressions; verify slope equivalence and use HAC intervals.

  3. Estimate joint time-block bootstrap confidence intervals for slope differences across all factors; control multiple comparisons (Holm or joint max statistic).

  4. Analyze 120-month rolling asymmetry and predefined crisis periods, reporting state counts.

  5. Repeat using market bottom/top terciles defined on training sample and examine sensitivity to extreme observations.

Minimum viable thesis: Five factors plus Mom, predeclared states, conditional slopes and means, uncertainty and subperiod checks. Distinct from A07.02.01, which tests cross-sectional pricing of downside beta.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
