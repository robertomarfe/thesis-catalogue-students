Goal

Liquidity-related spreads may reflect risk or correlated characteristics. Factor regressions and financial-condition interactions measure residual associations, not structural liquidity causation.

Research question: Are published liquidity-related long-short returns explained by standard factors, and do their residual returns vary with financial conditions?

Student-owned research

Pin same OSAP dictionary and return revision, exactly three named signals and direction; match factors/RF conventions and release-aware NFCI. Fit unconditional and interaction models on same dates; conditional alpha=a+cz, conditional beta=b+dz. Joint blocks and multiple-comparison sensitivity; defer if no authorized bytes.

  1. Freeze release, signal definitions, long/short orientation and portfolio formation metadata; inspect missingness without filling.

  2. Estimate mean returns, volatility, drawdowns (clearly funded hypothetical spread convention) and factor alphas with HAC.

  3. Test joint alpha restrictions with common-date block bootstrap, report Holm-adjusted individual tests.

  4. Estimate NFCI-state interactions and conditional alphas with same factors; compare stable subsamples.

  5. Apply transparent fixed cost hurdles as sensitivity, not estimated turnover costs, and compare alternative OSAP portfolio weighting if available.

Minimum viable thesis: Three prespecified liquid/illiquid-related long-short series and FF3/5+Mom alpha comparisons with inference and NFCI interaction. If files fail, hold assignment pending; substitution requires supervisor approval. Distinct from A12.01.02’s BAB mechanism.
