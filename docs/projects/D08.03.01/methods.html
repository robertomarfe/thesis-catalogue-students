Payoff and index return

Without dividends, parity gives S0−C0=Ke−rT−P0S_0-C_0=Ke^{-rT}-P_0. Equal payoff and funding require common strike, maturity and carry. A rolling index accounts for premium and roll methodology and is not an actual investor’s trading record.

Risk matching

A feasible equity weight is wt−1=σ̂t−1B/σ̂t−1Mw_{t-1}=\widehat\sigma^B_{t-1}/\widehat\sigma^M_{t-1} subject to declared bounds. Compare wt−1RtM+(1−wt−1)Rtfw_{t-1}R^M_t+(1-w_{t-1})R^f_t with BXM on common months. Use lagged rather than test-period risk estimates. Report achieved risk, upside/downside capture conditional on comparator sign, counts and paired uncertainty.
