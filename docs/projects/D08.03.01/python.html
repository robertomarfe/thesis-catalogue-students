Goal

Covered-call indices can display lower volatility because some equity upside is sold. Comparing with unscaled buy-and-hold confounds reduced risk exposure with insurance value. Evaluate against a feasible lagged risk-matched equity/cash portfolio.

Research question: How much upside does a covered-call benchmark sacrifice, and does its downside protection survive comparison with a volatility-matched equity/cash portfolio?

Student-owned research

This is historical buy-write evaluation, not synthetic hedge optimization. Gate remains blocked until BXM rights and history pass. Price-only SP500 is not an admissible comparator; no pure option-premium interpretation.

  1. Derive payoff and explain BXM roll/dividend methodology.

  2. Validate index levels, common end-month dates, returns and rebasing.

  3. Compare cumulative wealth, Sharpe, drawdown, tail losses and conditional upside/downside returns.

  4. Build equity/RF mix using trailing 60-month volatility ratio capped at one; compare on identical evaluation dates.

  5. Estimate market beta and up/down beta with HAC; bootstrap paired months in blocks; repeat live-only and cost scenarios.

Minimum viable thesis: BXM plus authorized equity total-return comparator/proxy, monthly paired returns, risk/capture and trailing volatility-matched baseline. If BXM unavailable, defer assignment; synthetic-only redesign needs approval.
