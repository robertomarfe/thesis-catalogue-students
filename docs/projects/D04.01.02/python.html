Goal

A delta hedge removes first-order stock exposure at the trading instant, but not option curvature between trades. The project asks whether a local curvature expansion explains the economically important part of replication errors and where it breaks down.

Research question: How accurately does a gamma-weighted quadratic-variation error explain discrete hedging losses across strikes and maturities, and when does the approximation break down?

Student-owned research

Distinguish explanatory accuracy from D04.01.01’s convergence study. Verify the sign on a single ledger, then report paired residuals, RMSE, variance ratio and tail errors normalized by spot as well as dollars.

  1. Derive Taylor/PDE cancellation and check sign on a one-step deterministic ledger.

  2. Simulate and compute hedge P&L, pathwise gamma approximation and its conditional variance sum.

  3. Compare approximation error, correlation, RMSE and variance ratio across moneyness/maturity cells.

  4. Plot time profiles of dollar gamma and error contribution, paying attention to near-expiry behavior.

  5. Test finer grids and volatility/drift changes; report independent-replication uncertainty and tail observations rather than dropping them.

Minimum viable thesis: Verified sign and cash ledger, three strikes, three maturities and three nested grids, quantitative approximation accuracy and failure diagnostics.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
