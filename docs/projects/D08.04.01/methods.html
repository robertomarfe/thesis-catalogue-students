Protective Puts and the Cost of Crash Insurance

How much downside protection does a systematic protective-put strategy provide relative to buy-and-hold and risk-matched stock-cash allocations, and at what realized return cost?

Premium financing

Holding one stock and borrowing the put premium gives WT=ST+(K−ST)+−P0erTW_T=S_T+(K-S_T)^+-P_0e^{rT} from net initial wealth S0S_0. This is not the repeated PPUT return. Index history depends on roll timing, strike, premium and methodology; it does not reveal individual option premiums.

Insurance value

Compare realized returns and tail loss on common months versus a lagged risk-matched stock-cash allocation. Drawdown depends on the wealth path; expected shortfall depends on return horizon.
