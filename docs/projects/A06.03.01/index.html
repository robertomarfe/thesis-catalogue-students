A06.03.01 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Equity Yield Slopes and the Sensitivity of Equity Portfolios to Interest Rates

Do portfolio-level model-implied equity yield slopes predict differential subsequent exposure to government yield changes, and does the association survive controls for investment style and market exposure?

Conditional data access · Level 3/3 · Research draft

Conditional data access. GKK archive and exact style-return matching (at least four styles).

OverviewResearch proposalTheory & methodsLiteratureData & sourcesPython labResearch plan

Research overview

Motivation

Rate sensitivity may depend on the timing of priced dividends and on equity valuation states. An interaction design tests this association without claiming that an observed rate movement is an exogenous discount-rate shock.

Minimum viable thesis

At least four matched style portfolios, 1/10Y slope, common sample return-rate interactions and joint inference plus theoretical duration example.

Prerequisites

Asset pricing, fixed income, panel econometrics, Python.

Methods

Cash-flow valuation · Fixed income & curves · Regression & inference · Time-series forecasting
