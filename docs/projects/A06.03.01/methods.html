A06.03.01 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Equity Yield Slopes and the Sensitivity of Equity Portfolios to Interest Rates

Research question

Do portfolio-level model-implied equity yield slopes predict differential subsequent exposure to government yield changes, and does the association survive controls for investment style and market exposure?

Conditional data access · Level 3/3 · Research draft

Conditional data access. GKK archive and exact style-return matching (at least four styles).

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

Let si,t−1=ei,t−1(10)−ei,t−1(1)s_{i,t-1}=e_{i,t-1}(10)-e_{i,t-1}(1) and s̃i,t−1\widetilde s_{i,t-1} be its within-date centered value.

Use asset and date effects in the primary regression, a slope main effect, and s̃i,t−1Δbt(10)\widetilde s_{i,t-1}\Delta b_t(10) as the differential rate-exposure regressor. Include relative portfolio market loadings; common market and rate main effects are collinear with date effects. Check design rank; the pooled asset-FE model without date effects is a secondary diagnostic. A coefficient times .01 gives the interaction sensitivity per 100bp times the slope difference in decimal units. Full-sample generated GKK yields prevent live forecasting claims.

Research checks

Prespecify pooled asset-FE diagnostic separately from primary time-FE specification. In latter omit common dy and market main effects, interact market with portfolio indicators as appropriate, center lagged slopes within date and check rank; include slope main effect, joint month block inference and exact t-1 availability. Require >=4 exactly mapped styles and scale effects per 100bp. Do not silently treat helper output as the primary panel test.
