Cash Flow News and Discount Rate News in Aggregate Stock Returns

How much of unexpected aggregate equity return variation is associated with discount-rate versus cash-flow news under alternative parsimonious VAR information sets?

Mathematical framework

For centered return-first VAR Xt+1=AXt+ut+1X_{t+1}=AX_t+u_{t+1}, discount-rate news at horizon HH is NDR=e1⊤∑j=1HρjAjut+1N_{DR}=e_1^\top\sum_{j=1}^H\rho^j A^j u_{t+1}.

Residual cash-flow news is NCF=e1⊤ut+1+NDRN_{CF}=e_1^\top u_{t+1}+N_{DR}, so return innovation equals NCF−NDRN_{CF}-N_{DR}. The variance identity includes −2Cov(NCF,NDR)-2\operatorname{Cov}(N_{CF},N_{DR}). Infinite discounted news uses ρA(I−ρA)−1\rho A(I-\rho A)^{-1} only when the discounted spectral radius is below one; require a stable VAR for the maintained stationary interpretation. Finite residual news absorbs terminal and approximation errors. Refit VAR and rho in resampling; report unstable draws. This is not an independently identified cash-flow shock.

Research checks

Use return-first VAR, stable roots, same calendar and real gross convention; verify direct discounted sums and signed Var(CF)+Var(DR)-2Cov identity, report finite and infinite estimates, horizon and information-set sensitivity. Refit rho and VAR inside bootstrap, retain inadmissible draws and note weak stability near unit roots.
