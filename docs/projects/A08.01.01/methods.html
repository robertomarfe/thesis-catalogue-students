Mean-Variance Optimization and the Cost of Estimation Error

Research question

How much does estimation error in expected returns and covariance reduce out-of-sample mean-variance portfolio performance relative to equal weights and minimum variance?

Mathematical framework

Maximize U(w)=w⊤μ−γw⊤Σw/2U(w)=w^\top\mu-\gamma w^\top\Sigma w/2 on the long-only simplex using monthly excess means and covariance.

True-moment oracle regret U(w*)−U(ŵ)≥0U(w^*)-U(\widehat w)\geq0 is distinct from realized sample CE. Cross true and estimated means and covariance on paired draws. Calculate wealth from total returns, Sharpe from excess returns; charge trading costs against drifted weights. Normal simple-return draws can imply nonpositive wealth; freeze admission policy and report failures.

Research checks
