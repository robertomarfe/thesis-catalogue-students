Mean and curvature

For dv=κ(θ−v)dt+ξvdWdv=\kappa(\theta-v)dt+\xi\sqrt v\,dW, the mean of VTV_T is m=θ+(v0−θ)(1−e−κT)/(κT)m=\theta+(v_0-\theta)(1-e^{-\kappa T})/(\kappa T). Taylor expansion of g(x)=xg(x)=\sqrt x at m>0m>0 yields 𝔼VT≈m−Var(VT)/(8m3/2).\mathbb E\sqrt{V_T}\approx\sqrt m-\operatorname{Var}(V_T)/(8m^{3/2}). Jensen’s bound is exact, not this local approximation. Wide or near-zero distributions can make the approximation inaccurate.

Numerical errors

Exact noncentral chi-square CIR transitions for ξ>0\xi>0 do not make the numerical integral exact. Handle ξ=0\xi=0 separately. Compare integration grids and distinguish sampling standard errors from time-integration bias.

Research checks

Unlike the VIX futures project, the random quantity is realized integrated variance over the entire swap horizon. Report economic errors in volatility points, not only variance units.
