Mathematical framework

Let aja_j denote the release timestamp of observation jj. At origin tt, zt=zj*(t)z_t=z_{j^*(t)} where j*(t)=max{j:aj≤t}j^*(t)=\max\{j:a_j\le t\} among observations actually available under the declared vintage. Estimate yt+1=a+bdpt+czt+et+1y_{t+1}=a+b dp_t+c z_t+e_{t+1} recursively and compare valuation-only and historical-mean forecasts on common dates. The coefficient cc is a predictive association. A latest-vintage reconstruction with lagged observations is pseudo-OOS, not a historical real-time dataset.
