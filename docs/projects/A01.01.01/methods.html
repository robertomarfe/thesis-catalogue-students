Testing the CAPM across Size and Value Portfolios

Research question

Does market beta account for differences in average excess returns across size and book-to-market portfolios? How sensitive is the conclusion to test assets and inference?

Market efficiency and pricing restrictions

Let xMx_M be market excess return and βi=Cov(xi,xM)/Var(xM)\beta_i=\operatorname{Cov}(x_i,x_M)/\operatorname{Var}(x_M). With the maintained mean-variance-efficient market proxy, αi=E[xi]−βiE[xM]=0\alpha_i=E[x_i]-\beta_iE[x_M]=0. Estimate xit=αi+βixMt+ϵitx_{it}=\alpha_i+\beta_ix_{Mt}+\epsilon_{it}. Fit a descriptive cross-sectional line x‾i=a+bβ̂i+ui\bar x_i=a+b\widehat\beta_i+u_i; the CAPM predicts a=0a=0 and b=E[xM]b=E[x_M]. Estimated betas are generated regressors, not error-free covariates.

Joint alpha inference

For TT dates, NN assets and KK factors, use covariance matrices Σ̂=T−1∑tϵ̂tϵ̂t⊤\widehat\Sigma=T^{-1}\sum_t\widehat\epsilon_t\widehat\epsilon_t^\top and Ω̂=T−1∑t(ft−f‾)(ft−f‾)⊤\widehat\Omega=T^{-1}\sum_t(f_t-\bar f)(f_t-\bar f)^\top. The classical GRS statistic is

T−N−KNα̂⊤Σ̂−1α̂1+f‾⊤Ω̂−1f‾.\frac{T-N-K}{N}\frac{\widehat\alpha^\top\widehat\Sigma^{-1}\widehat\alpha}{1+\bar f^\top\widehat\Omega^{-1}\bar f}.
The exact FN,T−N−KF_{N,T-N-K} reference uses the iid Gaussian regression benchmark and full ranks. Robust inference must respect serial and cross-asset dependence. High R2R^2 is not a zero-alpha test.

Research checks

Use one RF subtraction, common complete dates and separate portfolio panels. Report T, N, K, eigenvalues and failed draws. Re-estimate both regression passes in joint date blocks; do not interpret a rejected joint hypothesis as proof of inefficiency.
