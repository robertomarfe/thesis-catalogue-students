**Purpose.** Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

## Backward policy construction
For exercise dates $t_1,\ldots,t_m=T$, the terminal payoff is $g(S_T)=(K-S_T)^+$. At $t_j$ regress discounted future cashflows generated by the later fitted policy on functions of current spot. With later stopping time $\tau$, the target is $\mathbb E^{\mathbb Q}[e^{-r(\tau-t_j)}g(S_\tau)\mid S_j]$. Use scaled basis functions, stable QR/SVD solves and rank diagnostics; specify the in-the-money subset. Maturity is eligible; time zero is not in the stated contract.

## Independent policy evaluation
Freeze coefficients and exercise dates before test simulation. Decisions use only current information. The expected policy value is a lower bound for the optimum, but noisy estimates may exceed a numerical tree reference. Separate variation across training seeds, conditional test SE and tree error. Pair policy comparisons on one independent test set.

## Research checks

Do not use realized future cashflows in test decisions. Compare three basis settings and three training sizes after one validated case. Distinguish regression conditioning from stopping-policy performance.
