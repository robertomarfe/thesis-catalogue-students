# Reading guide | A05.03.02

Read in this order alongside the Methods Note; publisher metadata or source description checked 2026-11-25, not all full texts reviewed. Obtain lawful access through the library.

### 1. [Cochrane and Piazzesi (2005), Bond Risk Premia, AER 95, 138–160](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828053828581)
Start with the forward-rate return construction and the single predictive factor; compare the empirical restrictions with your own frozen-sample procedure, not the reported fit as a target.

### 2. [Fama and Bliss (1987), The Information in Long-Maturity Forward Rates, AER 77, 680–692](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v77y1987i4p680-92.html)
Use the forward-spread forecasting idea as an economic benchmark; distinguish expectations of future rates from time-varying premia.

### 3. [Gürkaynak, Sack and Wright (2007), The U.S. Treasury Yield Curve: 1961 to the Present, JME 54, 2291–2304](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm)
Understand why a fitted off-the-run zero curve is not a panel of directly observed zero prices, and check the quoted compounding convention.
