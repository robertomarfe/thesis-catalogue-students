Forward Rates and Time-Varying Bond Risk Premia

Research question

Does a training-estimated Cochrane-Piazzesi-style common forward factor improve multi-maturity bond risk-premium forecasts, and are its weights and predictive gains stable across time?

Stability and inference

Report weight profiles and predictions under the declared normalization; scaling gtg_t changes its coefficient, not the forecast. Compare unrestricted maturity regressions using equal information. Re-estimate both stages in any resampling procedure. Overlap, predictor persistence and limited regime sample sizes matter for uncertainty.

Research checks

Do not infer a structural factor from a good in-sample fit. Report fixed and expanding results separately, with actual eligible label counts and stability intervals.
