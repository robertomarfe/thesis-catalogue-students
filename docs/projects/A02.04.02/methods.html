Mathematical framework

For q∈{0.10,0.50,0.90}q\in\{0.10,0.50,0.90\}, estimate conditional quantile Qq(yt+1|xt)=aq+bq⊤xtQ_q(y_{t+1}|x_t)=a_q+b_q^\top x_t on matured data by minimizing ∑sρq(ys+1−Qq(xs))\sum_s\rho_q(y_{s+1}-Q_q(x_s)), where ρq(u)=u(q−𝟏{u<0})\rho_q(u)=u(q-\mathbf1\{u<0\}). Compare pinball loss against empirical historical quantiles and a rolling 60-month Gaussian location-scale benchmark calibrated only on available outcomes. Coverage is 1T∑t𝟏{yt+1≤Q̂q,t}\frac1T\sum_t\mathbf1\{y_{t+1}\le\widehat Q_{q,t}\}. Report crossing separately; a declared monotone rearrangement is a sensitivity rather than a hidden correction.

Identification and implementation

For an 80% central interval [l,u][l,u], the interval score is (u−l)+10(l−y)𝟏{y<l}+10(y−u)𝟏{y>u}(u-l)+10(l-y)\mathbf{1}\{y<l\}+10(y-u)\mathbf{1}\{y>u\}. Report marginal coverage and conditional exceedance behavior separately. VIX is an implied risk-neutral volatility indicator, not a physical quantile. Do not infer expected shortfall from three quantiles.

Research checks

Hand-check pinball loss for positive and negative residuals and empirical quantile convention. Check VIX availability and no future data. Test crossing detection, report event counts, no post-test choice of quantile or rearrangement.
