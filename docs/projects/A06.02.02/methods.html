A06.02.02 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Equity Yield Curves across Investment Styles

Research question

How do the level, slope and cyclicality of model-implied equity yields differ between high and low characteristic portfolios, especially value and growth?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

For an exactly matched high/low style pair define Δet(n)=eH,t(n)−eL,t(n)\Delta e_t(n)=e_{H,t}(n)-e_{L,t}(n).

The difference of 10–1 year slopes is Δet(10)−Δet(1)\Delta e_t(10)-\Delta e_t(1). Estimate uncertainty from joint blocks of dates, preserving all assets and maturities together. Positive-dividend portfolio claims are essential for log normalization; a long-short factor payoff is not such a claim. A slope difference cannot identify duration or discount-rate news separately from cash-flow expectations. Report labels, weights, units and vintage; no inferred French crosswalk.

Research checks

Require positive-dividend portfolio IDs and validated exact high/low style crosswalk with common 1/5/10y dates; report both individual curves and paired differences/slope, joint calendar-block intervals and sensitivity across regimes. Keep matched weighting, units and vintage. If exact pair not verified, stop rather than substitute FF labels.
