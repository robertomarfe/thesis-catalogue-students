Hansen-Jagannathan Distance and the Comparison of Asset Pricing Models

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Projection onto the pricing restrictions

For column XX of gross returns with unit prices and candidate mm, seek a correction hh minimizing E[h2]E[h^2] subject to E[hX]=−gE[hX]=-g. Decompose hh into its projection onto the payoff span and an orthogonal component. For full-rank G=E[XX⊤]G=E[XX^\top], the minimum correction is h=−X⊤G−1gh=-X^\top G^{-1}g, so d2=E[h2]=g⊤G−1gd^2=E[h^2]=g^\top G^{-1}g. This is an uncentered second moment, not a covariance. A singular matrix calls for an independent basis or an explicit range check; a ridge defines a different metric. The adjusted kernel need not be positive.

Fit m=a−b⊤fm=a-b^\top f using training pricing restrictions. Evaluate frozen coefficients on common held-out payoffs with the same GG for all candidates on that panel. Negative realizations and raw errors remain important diagnostics.

Research checks

Construct gross returns including the actual monthly bill, not excess returns with unit prices. Distinguish train and holdout metrics and apply common conditioning rules; paired time-block resampling should refit the training step and evaluate common holdout draws.
