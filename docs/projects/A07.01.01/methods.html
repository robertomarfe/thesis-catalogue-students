A07.01.01 · Asset Pricing · Volatility & tail risk

The Variance Risk Premium and the Predictability of Stock Market Returns

Research question

Does an ex ante 30-day variance risk premium proxy help forecast US equity excess returns over 1 and 3 months relative to a historical mean, and how sensitive is the result to the physical variance forecast and sample coverage?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Ex ante variance premium proxy

Qt,30Q_{t,30} is a VIX-based risk-neutral variance proxy, not a precise traded variance-swap quote. Estimate Pt,30P_{t,30} from forecasts of daily squared log SPX returns summed over scheduled sessions in the next 30 calendar days. Then Q−PQ-P is known at formation. Substituting realized future variance for PP creates a look-ahead variable; ex post differences may be reported separately.

Predictive regression

At month-end tt, form a target Rt,t+He=∏j=1H(1+Rm,t+j)−∏j=1H(1+Rf,t+j)R^e_{t,t+H}=\prod_{j=1}^H(1+R_{m,t+j})-\prod_{j=1}^H(1+R_{f,t+j}), where RmR_m is total simple market return and RfR_f is bill return. Estimate a historical mean and a regression on the lagged proxy at identical dates. Admit training labels only when their full horizon has matured. Use HAC or joint blocks for overlapping three-month outcomes; report one-SD effects and OOS loss, not just in-sample significance.
