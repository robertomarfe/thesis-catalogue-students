Goal

An option-implied variance measure combines expected variation and compensation for variance risk. The difference between risk-neutral and physical forecasts may contain information about expected equity returns. The study must distinguish genuine ex ante variation from a difference constructed using future realized variance.

Research question: Does an ex ante 30-day variance risk premium proxy help forecast US equity excess returns over 1 and 3 months relative to a historical mean, and how sensitive is the result to the physical variance forecast and sample coverage?

Setup check

Check the calendar-day scale of a toy annual variance without constructing a VRP or return forecast. This artificial example does not validate the full research design or actual data.

import numpy as np
annual_variance=.04; days=30; horizon=annual_variance*days/365
assert np.isclose(horizon*365/days,annual_variance)
print('Setup check passed.')

Student-owned research

The matched SPX source remains blocked until rights and actual coverage are confirmed. Short samples may support descriptive results only; long-proxy OOS analysis requires explicit supervisor agreement. No silent source substitution or claim of an intraday replication.

  1. Clean closes and calendar; compute trailing 252/21-day historical daily variance and forecast 30-calendar-day sum using only data known at t. Compare a fixed EWMA (lambda=0.94 daily) or GARCH as alternative; use a scheduled calendar and state missing-data rule.

  2. Construct horizon-matched VRP in decimal variance units; report descriptive statistics and verify annualization.

  3. Regress next 1- and 3-month compounded market-minus-bill returns on lagged VRP, with HAC for overlap; report effect of a one-SD predictor move and incremental R2.

  4. Build expanding-window forecasts against historical mean using identical dates; compare OOS R2 and error-difference uncertainty. Repeat long proxy sample separately, and never use realized future variation to construct predictors.

Minimum viable thesis: Reproducible ex ante VRP construction, explicit units/underlying distinction, 1- and 3-month predictive regressions and at least one chronological OOS comparison where sample permits. Null or unstable results are acceptable; no intraday or option-chain subscription required.
