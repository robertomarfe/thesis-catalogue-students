A06.02.01 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

The Term Structure of Equity Yields: Evidence from Synthetic Dividend Strips

Research question

What are the main level, slope and curvature patterns of US aggregate equity yields, and how do they differ in recessions versus expansions? Does subtracting nominal bond yields change the economic interpretation of the slope?

Conditional data access · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

For positive dividend normalization DtD_t, let Pt(n)P_t^{(n)} price the dividend at maturity nn. Define et(n)=−n−1log(Pt(n)/Dt)e_t(n)=-n^{-1}\log(P_t^{(n)}/D_t).

Match continuously compounded nominal zero yields bt(n)b_t(n) and equity yields only after checking the provider definitions. The difference ft(n)=et(n)−bt(n)f_t(n)=e_t(n)-b_t(n) is a bond-adjusted equity yield, not a forward rate between maturities and not an expected excess return: growth is embedded. Use centered PCA on complete common maturity dates with a fixed sign convention. Inference on generated curves is conditional on their estimation, not independent validation of strip prices.
