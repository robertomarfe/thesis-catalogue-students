Implied Volatility Surface Dynamics and Forecasting

Research question

Do dynamic factors of constant-maturity implied total variance outperform persistence and simple autoregressions for out-of-sample surface forecasts?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Fixed coordinates and data provenance

At each observation time tt, define total implied variance wt(k,T)=TIt(k,T)2w_t(k,T)=T I_t(k,T)^2 at fixed log-forward moneyness kk and constant maturity TT. Use contemporaneous forward and discount inputs and only supported strike/maturity interpolation within a snapshot. Keep observed versus interpolated indicators; never fill missing historical sessions using future data. Vendor-smoothed surfaces and raw quotes are different measurement objects, requiring distinct provenance and permission checks.

Forecasting the factor representation

Let xtx_t collect supported total-variance nodes, with training mean x‾\bar x and loading matrix LmL_m. Factor scores for levels are ft=Lm⊤(xt−x‾)f_t=L_m^\top(x_t-\bar x). An AR or ridge VAR predicts scores at a declared horizon hh, then reconstructs x̂t+h=x‾+Lmf̂t+h\hat x_{t+h}=\bar x+L_m\hat f_{t+h}. If using changes, reconstruction instead starts from the origin level and adds predicted changes over the horizon. Keep the two estimands separate. Compare persistence and nodewise AR on identical origin-target pairs, with rank and penalties chosen on validation only and purge labels overlapping split boundaries. Report per-node losses, paired block uncertainty, and violations of finite-grid forecast arbitrage restrictions; any projection is a distinct method. Adequate history and authorized access are still blocked.

Research checks

Do not replace missing market history with a synthetic study without approval. Freeze the supported nodes, complete session calendar, chronological boundaries and purge. Preserve measurement masks and compare the same targets, including failed or arbitrage-violating forecasts.
