Reading focus: Incremental forecasting content of news EPU beyond lagged RV and VIX, not a general model interpretability study.

News EPU and composite EPU are different measures. Using a complete month index before its publication creates leakage; VIX is implied risk-neutral volatility and its square is not realized physical variance without an explicit horizon and units convention. Revised EPU series do not establish vintage availability.
