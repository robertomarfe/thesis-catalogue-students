Reading focus: Break-even cost/turnover combinations for published anomaly returns and alphas, not actual execution costs.

Published long-short returns are not funded wealth series. Hypothetical turnover is a convention (one-way versus absolute dollars, long and short legs); subtracting constant monthly costs shifts intercept by that constant on identical regression rows but variable costs may change slopes. Negative break-even values mean gross expected loss, not a negative feasible trading cost.
