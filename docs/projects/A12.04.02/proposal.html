Asset pricing · Portfolio choice, behavioral finance and market frictions · Level 2/3

Data: Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Gross anomaly returns do not establish tradability. Scenario analysis quantifies the cost and turnover combinations that absorb published returns or alpha.

Research question

For a preselected set of published anomalies, what combinations of turnover and per-dollar trading costs eliminate gross mean returns or factor-adjusted alpha?

Economic mechanism

With constant monthly turnover τ>0\tau>0 and cost cc, rtnet=rt−cτr_t^{net}=r_t-c\tau; mean break-even cost is r‾/τ\bar r/\tau for nonnegative gross mean.

Research design

Eight prespecified OSAP signals, matching dictionary, FF3/5+Mom, 1990–2025 target, fixed split; turnover .1/.25/.5/1/2 and 5–100bp, paired mean/alpha uncertainty.

Pin eight named signals and OSAP dictionary, common factor sample; report cost/turnover grid with exact units, negative alphas and uncertainty. Verify intercept translation with constant costs; use exact matched turnover only when authorized and documented, else scenario exercise. Do not compound unfunded spread or claim tradability.

Required exhibits

Break-even cost/turnover contours, gross and scenario-net alpha table, data/provenance audit and uncertainty.

Minimum viable thesis

Eight signals, common factor sample, two-dimensional cost grid and explicit no-turnover-data limitation.

Scope and feasibility

Return-only series cannot recover traded notional, spreads, market impact, borrow or capacity. Scenario analysis is not historical executable net performance. No turnover inferred from return autocorrelation. Avoid look-ahead signal selection.

Prerequisites: Factor models, portfolio accounting, regression, Python.

Optional extension: Use verified published holdings/turnover for a subset, then compare scenario and observed turnover-based estimates.

Milestones

W1-3: source/turnover audit; W4-6: gross benchmarks; W7-9: cost grids; W10-12: alpha and uncertainty; W13-16: writing.
