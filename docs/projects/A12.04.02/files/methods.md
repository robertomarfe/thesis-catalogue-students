**Purpose.** Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

## Mathematical framework

With constant monthly turnover $\tau>0$ and cost $c$, $r_t^{net}=r_t-c\tau$; mean break-even cost is $\bar r/\tau$ for nonnegative gross mean.

With an intercept and identical factor rows a constant deduction shifts alpha exactly, leaving slopes unchanged. Variable costs may change slopes. Negative mean or alpha has no nonnegative break-even; distinguish cost units and both trading legs. Verify gross/net provider convention. Without matching turnover, scenario only; do not compound unfunded spreads into wealth.

## Research checks

Pin eight named signals and OSAP dictionary, common factor sample; report cost/turnover grid with exact units, negative alphas and uncertainty. Verify intercept translation with constant costs; use exact matched turnover only when authorized and documented, else scenario exercise. Do not compound unfunded spread or claim tradability.
