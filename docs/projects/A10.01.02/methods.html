Predicting Factor Returns with Machine Learning

Research question

Do ridge and LASSO forecasts of monthly SMB, HML and momentum improve over factor-specific historical means and fixed OLS models in a genuinely held-out period?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

For k∈{SMB,HML,MOM}k\in\{SMB,HML,MOM\}, predict fk,t+1f_{k,t+1} with an individual training-only ridge or LASSO fit. Define dk,t=Lmean,k,t−Lmodel,k,td_{k,t}=L_{mean,k,t}-L_{model,k,t}, keeping the vector dtd_t intact in date-block resampling to preserve cross-factor dependence. Factor-specific historical means use matured factor returns only. Factor returns are long-short payoffs, not automatically funded excess-return investment strategies; collateral and financing require a separate convention.

Identification and implementation

For model jj define ROS,k,j2=1−SSEk,j/SSEk,0R^2_{OS,k,j}=1-\mathrm{SSE}_{k,j}/\mathrm{SSE}_{k,0}. Joint date blocks retain correlation of the three loss differences. A simultaneous confidence region or prespecified multiplicity adjustment distinguishes individual from family-wide evidence. Do not subtract RF from zero-cost factor payoffs.

Research checks

Check factor signs, dates, percent scaling, source vintage and lags. Keep all three factor loss gains together in resampling. Freeze OLS specification; negative R2 and weak gains allowed; no selected winner p-value.
