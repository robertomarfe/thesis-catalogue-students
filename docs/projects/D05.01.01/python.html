D05.01.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Monte Carlo pricing is attractive for path-dependent claims, but a small standard error need not imply computational efficiency. Variance reduction changes the sampling procedure rather than the pricing model. The project asks which methods save computation for the same payoff and why the gains depend on moneyness and averaging.

Research question: Which of antithetic variates, control variates and their combination yields the best pricing precision per unit runtime for European and discretely monitored Asian calls? Does the efficiency ranking depend on moneyness?

Student-owned research

Freeze the payoff, seed and timing protocol. The combined estimator addresses the full question even when the MVT requires only two methods. Do not label a high-budget arithmetic reference exact.

  1. Implement plain MC and validate European prices against BSM; derive and test the geometric Asian price against plain MC.

  2. Implement antithetics and control variates using pilot-estimated coefficients; compare identical path evaluations, include pilot overhead.

  3. Estimate bias, empirical variance, RMSE and 95% interval coverage across seeds; compare runtime and variance-times-runtime efficiency.

  4. Report log-log RMSE convergence and efficiency by payoff and moneyness. For Asian benchmark include its numerical uncertainty or evaluate paired differences.

Minimum viable thesis: Two payoffs, plain MC plus two variance-reduction methods, independent replications, known analytic European benchmark, and a timing-normalized comparison. A ranking reversal across strikes is a useful finding.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
