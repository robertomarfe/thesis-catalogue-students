Variance Reduction in Monte Carlo Option Pricing

Which of antithetic variates, control variates and their combination yields the best pricing precision per unit runtime for European and discretely monitored Asian calls? Does the efficiency ranking depend on moneyness?

Sampling and controls

Under the pricing measure ℚ\mathbb Q, exact GBM log increments over hh have mean (r−q−σ2/2)h(r-q-\sigma^2/2)h and variance σ2h\sigma^2h, where r,q,σr,q,\sigma are rate, dividend yield and volatility. The price is 𝔼ℚX\mathbb E^{\mathbb Q}X for discounted payoff XX. Antithetic normals Z,−ZZ,-Z produce one independent pair average. A control YY with known mean μY\mu_Y gives the adjusted payoff X−b(Y−μY)X-b(Y-\mu_Y); minimizing variance yields b*=Cov(X,Y)/Var(Y)b^*=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(Y) for positive control variance. Estimate bb on independent pilot paths to preserve conditional unbiasedness. Discounted stock has mean S0e−qTS_0e^{-qT}. The geometric Asian payoff is a control for the arithmetic claim; the Gaussian log-average variance is σ2m−2∑i,jmin(ti,tj)\sigma^2m^{-2}\sum_{i,j}\min(t_i,t_j) for mm fixings.

Precision and cost

Compare equal raw payoff evaluations and include pilot overhead. Assess coverage against the analytic European reference and RMSE relative to an independent arithmetic reference with its own uncertainty. Count pairs rather than treating their members as independent. Variance times runtime measures cost-adjusted precision; report absolute errors where ratios are unstable.

Research checks

Freeze the payoff, seed and timing protocol. The combined estimator addresses the full question even when the MVT requires only two methods. Do not label a high-budget arithmetic reference exact.
