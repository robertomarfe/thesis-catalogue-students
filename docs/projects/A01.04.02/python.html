Goal

Principal components extract directions of high return variance, whereas an asset-pricing model must explain mean returns. An equal-dimensional, frozen comparison on later dates and distinct portfolio constructions separates covariance reconstruction from pricing.

Research question: At equal factor dimension, do principal-component factors estimated on training assets produce smaller held-out pricing errors than observable FF factors?

Student-owned research

Freeze factor dimensions, weights, means and model selection before the test. Audit date and asset holdouts separately, report factor return scaling and rank, and retain common pricing moments. Optional OSAP alternative requires its own access verification.

  1. Audit excess returns, common dates and covariance.

  2. Fit PCs using training assets only, verify orthogonality and sign invariance.

  3. Fit each pricing model on training assets and dates.

  4. Evaluate same-asset future and disjoint-portfolio future pricing errors, alongside variance explained.

  5. Compare rolling re-estimation, correlation PCA and factor dimension sensitivity using training-only rules.

Minimum viable thesis: PC3 and FF3 on French panels, one frozen date/asset holdout and pricing versus covariance comparison.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
