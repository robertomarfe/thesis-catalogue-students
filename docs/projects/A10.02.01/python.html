Goal

Shallow ensembles can capture thresholds and interactions, but financial time series contain few independent observations. The fair test gives nonlinear and linear models the same information and honest selection budget.

Research question: Do shallow tree ensembles provide incremental out-of-sample equity-premium forecast accuracy relative to linear models under identical information and model-selection rules?

Student-owned research

No random cross-validation; identical dates/features and mature label rules; perturb future inputs and labels; verify depth/minimum leaf constraints; record seeds and failed fits, not only successful winners.

  1. Reproduce expanding mean and ridge; audit feature timing and target dates.

  2. Implement expanding RF and gradient boosting with inner walk-forward folds; select hyperparameters by past-only MSE, never final-period outcomes.

  3. Compare final loss, OOS R2, forecast correlation, paired block-bootstrap uncertainty and subperiods.

  4. Inspect partial dependence or validation permutation importance for a few prespecified features, cautioning correlated predictors.

  5. Evaluate bounded market weight from forecasts and past variance against static allocation, with transaction costs and consistent funding.

Minimum viable thesis: Expanding mean, ridge and one shallow ensemble, nested chronological tuning, untouched holdout loss table and uncertainty. Distinct from A10.01.01 linear regularization and A10.04.02 search-bias simulations.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
