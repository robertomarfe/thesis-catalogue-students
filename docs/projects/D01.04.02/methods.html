Scaling and numerical uncertainty

Define the centered near-ATM slope using strikes k=±cTk=\pm c\sqrt T in log-forward units. In the model’s applicable short-time asymptotic regime the magnitude of skew has leading power TH−1/2T^{H-1/2}, with sign governed by the correlation for the chosen parameters. Estimate the log-log slope only on prespecified maturities with reliable nonzero skew. Monte Carlo noise in both prices and their implied-vol inversion can dominate a small strike difference. Use paired simulation, error bars and grid refinement; check the discounted stock mean. The model’s finite-horizon predictions need not follow the asymptotic power exactly.

Research checks

Validate the covariance and cross-covariance before simulating prices. Keep the initial variance curve and other parameters fixed across H. Separate grid refinement, sampling uncertainty and finite-maturity asymptotic bias, with a Brownian-kernel reference.
