Student-owned research

  1. Derive projection, orthogonality and normalization; verify pricing residual and rank. 2. Generate incomplete/complete economies, compute minimum-norm and positive alternative SDFs, show common pricing. 3. Add assets sequentially, compare projection variance, maximum attainable Sharpe and equality conditions. 4. Construct cases where unrestricted minimum-norm SDF is negative despite an arbitrage-free positive alternative; solve constrained minimum-norm and compare. 5. Perturb payoffs/prices to examine numerical conditioning and redundant assets; test invariance to basis change and scaling.

Minimum viable thesis: Two nested finite-state economies, exact pricing and projection, null-space alternatives, positivity and Sharpe geometry with numerical verification.
