Goal

Negative equity returns are often followed by more volatile markets than equally large positive returns. A symmetric variance recursion cannot capture this asymmetry. The research separates the shape of the news-impact function from the ability of an estimated model to predict future variation.

Research question: Do GJR-GARCH and EGARCH improve next-day and multi-day variance forecasts relative to symmetric GARCH, especially after negative market innovations?

Setup check

Check equal absolute size and opposite sign of artificial innovations, without estimating a variance model. This artificial example does not validate the full research design or actual data.

assert (u<0).tolist()==[True,False]
print('Setup check passed.')

Student-owned research

Plot news-impact curves at a common prior variance. Report optimization failures and standardized residual checks. Sign-specific forecast improvement is predictive evidence, not proof of financial leverage as a causal mechanism. Use paired time blocks or HAC for overlapping outcomes.

  1. Inspect tails, autocorrelation of squares and sign-conditioned variance; specify constant or zero mean once.

  2. Estimate symmetric GARCH, GJR and EGARCH with common likelihood and report convergence and admissibility.

  3. Plot news-impact curves at common previous variance; compare response to equal positive and negative innovations.

  4. Produce genuine chronological 1- and 5-day forecasts, using simulation for nonlinear EGARCH multi-step expectations.

  5. Compare QLIKE and MSE against symmetric GARCH and rolling variance, overall and by sign of last innovation; use HAC or block bootstrap for overlapping loss differences.

Minimum viable thesis: Three fitted models with admissibility checks, news-impact analysis and chronological loss comparison at one horizon (plus five-day as standard extension), including uncertainty and convergence reporting.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
