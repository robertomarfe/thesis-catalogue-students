## Motivation

Survey confidence may add predictive information beyond valuation ratios. Confidence changes are not structural innovations and raw and smoothed series cannot be interchanged.

## Research question

Do lagged changes in individual and institutional one-year confidence predict subsequent market excess returns beyond valuation ratios?

## Economic mechanism

Let $x_t=C_t-C_{t-1}$ be the change of authorized one-year confidence in percentage points. Forecast next-month excess market return using released $x$ and lagged $dp$.

## Research design

Authorized raw monthly Yale one-year confidence (one population sufficient for MVT), Goyal dp and French excess market; fixed warm-up and lag; no unauthorized redistribution.

Obtain permission and exact monthly series/population/horizon; verify calendar and dates, predeclare lag and extra-lag sensitivity. One authorized series suffices for MVT; compare dp baseline and past-only historical mean on same origins, with paired blocks; defer if access or sample insufficient.

## Required exhibits

Survey definition and timing table, confidence series plots if permitted, predictive estimates and OOS R2, group and lag sensitivity.

## Minimum viable thesis

One authorized one-year confidence series, market return and valuation control, lagged forecast test with explicit timing uncertainty. Defer if data or rights unavailable; alternative sentiment proxy needs approval.

## Scope and feasibility

Raw monthly differs from legacy smoothed data; pre-2001 sparse surveys not monthly. No causal claims; survey populations not all investors. Limited independent 12-month windows. Yale redistribution gate mandatory.

**Prerequisites:** Predictive regressions, survey measurement, HAC, Python.

**Optional extension:** Compare crash confidence and buy-on-dips indexes with prespecified multiplicity control.
