Research plan | A12.02.01

Study design and independent work

Obtain permission and exact monthly series/population/horizon; verify calendar and dates, predeclare lag and extra-lag sensitivity. One authorized series suffices for MVT; compare dp baseline and past-only historical mean on same origins, with paired blocks; defer if access or sample insufficient.

Prespecified implementation

Use raw One-Year Confidence for individuals and institutions, separate populations; monthly since July 2001, not legacy six-month moving average. Confirm actual publication date or lag at least full month and label pseudo-OOS; sample likely short, predeclare initial training (e.g. 120 months if supported), avoid post-hoc splits. Goyal dp preceding month and French market excess; confidence index percentage points not returns, standardize training only; historical mean, dp, confidence and joint, same origins with lagged predictors; report HAC and paired block uncertainty. No data reproduction/distribution without written Yale permission; publish only authorized summaries.

Required tasks

  1. Confirm free index definitions, frequency, sample and permitted use; graph raw and trailing-six-month means separately.

  2. Align survey dates and return windows; examine individual/institutional correlation and measurement precision.

  3. Estimate 1/12-month predictive regressions in levels/changes with dp control, HAC and block bootstrap.

  4. Evaluate expanding mean and dp-only vs confidence models with initial 120 months, matured labels and same dates.

  5. Repeat lag 2/3 months and compare respondent groups; treat findings as associations.

Required exhibits

Survey definition and timing table, confidence series plots if permitted, predictive estimates and OOS R2, group and lag sensitivity.

Robustness

Raw monthly differs from legacy smoothed data; pre-2001 sparse surveys not monthly. No causal claims; survey populations not all investors. Limited independent 12-month windows. Yale redistribution gate mandatory.

Minimum viable thesis

One authorized one-year confidence series, market return and valuation control, lagged forecast test with explicit timing uncertainty. Defer if data or rights unavailable; alternative sentiment proxy needs approval.

Optional extension

Compare crash confidence and buy-on-dips indexes with prespecified multiplicity control.

Prerequisites

Predictive regressions, survey measurement, HAC, Python.

Thesis outline

  1. Beliefs and asset pricing;

  2. Survey measurement;

  3. Data/timing;

  4. Predictive evidence;

  5. Limitations.

Milestones

W1-3: source/rights gate; W4-6: alignment and theory; W7-9: regressions; W10-12: OOS and sensitivity; W13-16: write.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
