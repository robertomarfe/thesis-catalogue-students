Asset pricing · Fixed income and interest rates · Level 2/3

Data: Empirical core; actual rights, coverage and input validation pending. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Global rate shocks can move different sovereign curves together, while local macro conditions affect each curve’s shape. A common-date comparison reveals whether conditional co-movement is robust across two distinct government-bond markets without asserting that they have identical risk-free rates.

Research question

How similar are the level, slope and curvature dynamics of US Treasury and euro-area AAA government curves across the business cycle, and does conditioning on local activity alter cross-market co-movement?

Economic mechanism

Use fixed-maturity summaries L=y10L=y_{10}, S=y10−y2S=y_{10}-y_2, and C=2y5−y2−y10C=2y_5-y_2-y_{10}.

Research design

Match the prescribed US Treasury and euro-area AAA fitted spot curves on the same last common monthly date. Relate curve summaries and changes to local industrial-production growth, then compare conditional correlations and their uncertainty across predetermined activity states.

Unlike A05.02.02, the primary target is economic conditioning of co-movement, not eigenspace stability. Report bin counts, uncertainty and any stale-date exclusions.

Required exhibits

Matched curve summaries, factor/loading panels, rolling correlation and conditional association table, common-sample/episode sensitivity.

Minimum viable thesis

Common 1/2/5/10Y curves, local activity proxies, level/slope/curvature changes and one conditional co-movement comparison with intervals.

Scope and feasibility

US Treasury and ECB AAA curves have different issuer/composition/liquidity, no currency-converted bond returns. No causal monetary policy claims; Eurostat series may be revised. Few stress episodes limit precision. Distinct from A05.02.02, which focuses on PCA dimensionality rather than macro-conditioned co-movement.

Prerequisites: Fixed income, macroeconomics, PCA, regression and time series.

Optional extension: Compare all-rated euro-area curve with AAA using matched sample, explicitly treating composition/credit as a difference rather than a risk-free benchmark.

Milestones

W1-3: providers and definitions; W4-6: match and factor tests; W7-9: comparisons; W10-12: state and episode robustness; W13-16: writing.
