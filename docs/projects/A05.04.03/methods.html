A05.04.03 · Asset Pricing · Fixed income & interest rates

The Dynamics of the Yield Curve over the Business Cycle: US and European Evidence

Research question

How similar are the level, slope and curvature dynamics of US Treasury and euro-area AAA government curves across the business cycle, and does conditioning on local activity alter cross-market co-movement?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Comparable curve objects

Use continuous nominal zero yields in decimal annual units, converting changes to bp for risk comparisons. A fixed area definition for euro-area industrial production and exact issuer class for the ECB curve are needed. Do not mix par and spot yields or AAA and all-rated curves. Changes require adjacent monthly observations.

Conditional co-movement

Compute correlations of the common-date changes in L,S,CL,S,C within predeclared activity bins, using local growth measured by the same reference-period convention. For predictive extensions use actual release information and lag both markets consistently. Block resampling should keep dates and cross-market pairs together. Changing composition, common factors and policy regimes may explain differences; correlation alone does not identify a structural shock or integration.

Research checks

Unlike A05.02.02, the primary target is economic conditioning of co-movement, not eigenspace stability. Report bin counts, uncertainty and any stale-date exclusions.
