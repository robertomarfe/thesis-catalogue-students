# Research plan | A05.04.03

## Study design and independent work

Unlike A05.02.02, the primary target is economic conditioning of co-movement, not eigenspace stability. Report bin counts, uncertainty and any stale-date exclusions.

## Required tasks

1. Audit curve keys, percent units, common month-end and macro region definitions.

2. Plot levels and compute matched L/S/C and their monthly changes.

3. Compare PCA subspaces fitted on early sample and reconstruct later dates; report residuals and sign convention.

4. Estimate conditional correlations and regressions of change factors on lagged local activity with interactions and paired blocks.

5. Stress sensitivity to predeclared 2008/2020 episodes, low-rate periods, sample matching and 5Y vs 10Y slope.

## Required exhibits

Matched curve summaries, factor/loading panels, rolling correlation and conditional association table, common-sample/episode sensitivity.

## Robustness

US Treasury and ECB AAA curves have different issuer/composition/liquidity, no currency-converted bond returns. No causal monetary policy claims; Eurostat series may be revised. Few stress episodes limit precision. Distinct from A05.02.02, which focuses on PCA dimensionality rather than macro-conditioned co-movement.

## Minimum viable thesis

Common 1/2/5/10Y curves, local activity proxies, level/slope/curvature changes and one conditional co-movement comparison with intervals.

## Optional extension

Compare all-rated euro-area curve with AAA using matched sample, explicitly treating composition/credit as a difference rather than a risk-free benchmark.

## Prerequisites

Fixed income, macroeconomics, PCA, regression and time series.

## Thesis outline

1. International term structure;

2. Curve factors and macro states;

3. Cross-market harmonization;

4. Conditional evidence;

5. Limits.

## Milestones

W1-3: providers and definitions; W4-6: match and factor tests; W7-9: comparisons; W10-12: state and episode robustness; W13-16: writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
