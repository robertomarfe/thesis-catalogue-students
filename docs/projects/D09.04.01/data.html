D09.04.01 · Derivatives · Credit & incomplete markets

Counterparty Credit Risk and the Pricing of Uncollateralized Derivatives

Research question

How do maturity, market volatility, hazard and recovery jointly shape unilateral CVA for an uncollateralized forward, and how sensitive is independence-based CVA to wrong-way risk?

Input contract

Synthetic equity forward S0=100, K=100exp((r-q)T), r=.02, q=.01, sigma=.15-.40, T=1,3,5 years, h=.005-.05, R=.4. Analytic EPE plus quadrature; independent exact GBM Monte Carlo on time grid and independently sampled exponential default times. Required wrong-way-risk comparison at one maturity: adapted Cox intensity with a deterministic baseline fitted to the independent survival curve on the grid; compare separately with fixed baseline. GSW optional illustration only.
