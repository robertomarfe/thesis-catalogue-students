Student-owned research

  1. Derive forward value and EPE via BSM. 2. Check h=0, R=1, sigma=0 and short/long sign conventions. 3. Compare quadrature and MC with confidence intervals. 4. Map exposure and CVA to maturity, hazard and recovery; compare fixed-hazard versus fixed-credit-spread scenarios. 5. Stress wrong-way dependence and report marginal default probabilities separately. Add one maturity with Cox intensity depending on the preceding underlying price. Calibrate a deterministic baseline sequentially to match the independent marginal survival curve at every grid node. Use separate calibration and evaluation paths; compare signed dependence and fixed-baseline cases, refine the grid and check with independent default-time simulation.

Minimum viable thesis: One equity forward, independent default, analytic exposure and independent Monte Carlo check, four hazard/volatility scenarios; at one maturity, compare independent and two signed adapted Cox-intensity cases after matching the marginal survival curve on a common grid. Report default probabilities, CVA, simulation and time-grid uncertainty and the separate fixed-baseline comparison.
