Goal

High volatility and uncertainty about volatility are distinct phenomena. VVIX measures option-implied variation of the volatility index; its incremental forecasting value should be assessed after controlling for the prevailing VIX level and past market variance. The focus is calibrated tail-event probabilities.

Research question: Does VVIX incrementally forecast extreme physical volatility over the next month after controlling for VIX and lagged realized variance?

Student-owned research

Do not treat VVIX as realized equity variance. Report all nested comparisons on identical matured dates, label rights and backfill risks, and use predeclared threshold sensitivity. Retain failed or single-class training fits.

  1. Audit calendars, units, missing quotes and label maturity.

  2. Construct unconditional and VIX/RV logistic benchmarks.

  3. Add VVIX under identical expanding origins and training-only regularization.

  4. Compare paired proper scores, reliability and uncertainty by block resampling.

  5. Stress 75/90th thresholds, leave crisis years out of descriptive score tables and compare continuous RV forecasts.

Minimum viable thesis: Two nested logistic forecasts plus unconditional benchmark, common dates and at least two proper scores.
