Continuous crossing

For xt=log(Vt/H)x_t=\log(V_t/H) under ℚ\mathbb Q, dx=(r−σV2/2)dt+σVdWdx=(r-\sigma_V^2/2)dt+\sigma_V dW. Conditional on positive endpoints x,yx,y over length hh, the probability of a hit is exp[−2xy/(σV2h)]\exp[-2xy/(\sigma_V^2h)]; if either endpoint is nonpositive, a hit is certain. The conditional bridge removes the constant drift. Compare this with the unconditional first-passage law obtained by reflection. For x0>0x_0>0 and drift mm, survival to TT equals

Φ(x0+mTσVT)−e−2mx0/σV2Φ(−x0+mTσVT).\Phi\left(\frac{x_0+mT}{\sigma_V\sqrt T}\right)-e^{-2mx_0/\sigma_V^2}\Phi\left(\frac{-x_0+mT}{\sigma_V\sqrt T}\right).

Recovery contract

For maturity-contingent recovery, the terminal payment is RFRF on an early hit and min(VT,F)\min(V_T,F) otherwise. Price the entire terminal payment at e−rTe^{-rT}. A recovery paid at the hitting date is a different instrument. Use RF≤H<FRF\le H<F as prescribed; do not infer a general ordering across recovery rules.

Research checks

Show the analytic hit check, bridge versus endpoint-only monitoring and common-path paired intervals. Compare the maturity-only benchmark under identical terminal inputs. Preserve unsuccessful or high-error grid cases and quantify both default timing and loss effects.
