The Black-Scholes Model and the Economic Implications of Its Assumptions

How do volatility, interest rates, dividends and time to maturity change European option prices and Greeks? How large are the pricing differences when the lognormal assumption is relaxed while matching forward and terminal variance?

Replication and local risk

Let tt be valuation time, TT expiry, and τ=T−t\tau=T-t. Write D=e−rτD=e^{-r\tau}, F=Ste(r−q)τF=S_te^{(r-q)\tau}, with continuously compounded rate rr, proportional dividend yield qq, and volatility σ\sigma. For a continuously traded stock with dividends, a self-financing replicating portfolio holds CSC_S shares and invests the remainder in the money market. Equating the drift of the derivative and portfolio gives Ct+(r−q)SCS+12σ2S2CSS−rC=0.C_t+(r-q)S C_S+\tfrac12\sigma^2 S^2C_{SS}-rC=0. The terminal payoff is (ST−K)+(S_T-K)^+, where KK is strike. With w=σ2τw=\sigma^2\tau and d1,2=log(F/K)/w±w/2d_{1,2}=\log(F/K)/\sqrt w\pm\sqrt w/2, the call price is D[FΦ(d1)−KΦ(d2)]D[F\Phi(d_1)-K\Phi(d_2)]. Here Φ\Phi is the standard normal cdf and ϕ\phi its density. Put–call parity is C−P=D(F−K)C-P=D(F-K).

Moment matching and sensitivity units

For σ,τ>0\sigma,\tau>0, delta is e−qτΦ(d1)e^{-q\tau}\Phi(d_1) and gamma is e−qτϕ(d1)/(Sστ)e^{-q\tau}\phi(d_1)/(S\sigma\sqrt\tau). Vega is Se−qτϕ(d1)τSe^{-q\tau}\phi(d_1)\sqrt\tau per unit decimal volatility; multiply by 0.01 for one volatility point. Rho is ∂C/∂r\partial C/\partial r, and calendar-time theta is ∂C/∂t\partial C/\partial t at fixed expiry. Derive these remaining Greeks and check signs and units against your own finite differences.

A two-component lognormal mixture with probabilities p,1−pp,1-p, conditional means both FF, and log variances a,ba,b has 𝔼ℚ[ST2]/F2=pea+(1−p)eb\mathbb E^{\mathbb Q}[S_T^2]/F^2=pe^a+(1-p)e^b. Match this to ewe^w by solving for bb; reject an inadmissible variance. This is not matching log-return variance. Compare with independent payoff integration; do not interpret the mixture as a dynamic replication model.

Research checks

Separate each input perturbation from a change in the terminal law. Hold other inputs fixed for Greeks; for the mixture preserve the forward and arithmetic second moment at every maturity. Report units and distinguish small perturbations from scenario repricing.
