Optimal Rebalancing Frequency with Transaction Costs

Research question

How does the optimal discrete delta-hedging frequency depend on transaction costs, volatility, maturity and the hedger's risk tolerance? Is the optimal frequency robust to model misspecification?

Policy choice

Prespecify the risk criterion and candidate calendars. Evaluate mean loss, dispersion, cost and uncertainty on independent paths, retaining near ties. An optimum is conditional on the objective and feasible grid.

Research checks
