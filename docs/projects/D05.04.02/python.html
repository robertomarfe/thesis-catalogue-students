Student-owned research

  1. Derive estimators and analytic references; verify units. 2. Implement independent seed replications and antithetic controls. 3. Compare bias, empirical SD, RMSE, CI coverage and runtime for increasing paths. 4. Inspect bump bias-variance and near-expiry/OTM cases. 5. Explain invalid digital pathwise result and quantify Asian reference uncertainty. For one arithmetic Asian call with 12 fixings, derive the likelihood-ratio score from all conditional log-increment densities and differentiate all fixings for pathwise vega. Use independent reference simulations with uncertainty; do not rank the invalid naive digital pathwise estimator.

Minimum viable thesis: European call and digital delta and vega, and one arithmetic Asian call (12 fixings, one strike and maturity), using bump-and-revalue, pathwise and likelihood-ratio estimators where valid. Include analytic European benchmarks, an independently generated numerical Asian reference with uncertainty and bump-size diagnostics, repeated seeds and a cost-normalized RMSE comparison. Explicitly report the invalid digital pathwise estimator rather than rank it.
