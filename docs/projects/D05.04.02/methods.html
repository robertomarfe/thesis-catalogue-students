1. European reference sensitivities

Write D=e−rTD=e^{-rT}, Z=WT/T∼N(0,1)Z=W_T/\sqrt T\sim N(0,1), and let Φ\Phi and ϕ\phi denote the standard normal CDF and density. Set d1=log(S0/K)+(r−q+σ2/2)TσT,d2=d1−σT.d_1=\frac{\log(S_0/K)+(r-q+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T},\qquad d_2=d_1-\sigma\sqrt T. A call has ΔC=e−qTΦ(d1)\Delta_C=e^{-qT}\Phi(d_1) and 𝒱C=S0e−qTϕ(d1)T\mathcal V_C=S_0e^{-qT}\phi(d_1)\sqrt T. A cash-or-nothing digital has VD=DΦ(d2)V_D=D\Phi(d_2), so that ΔD=Dϕ(d2)S0σT,𝒱D=−Dϕ(d2)d1σ.\Delta_D=\frac{D\phi(d_2)}{S_0\sigma\sqrt T},\qquad \mathcal V_D=-\frac{D\phi(d_2)d_1}{\sigma}. These formulas provide independent reference values for the European experiment.

2. Bump, pathwise and likelihood ratio

For parameter a∈{S0,σ}a\in\{S_0,\sigma\} and relative bump b>0b>0, use Ĝb=D[H(a(1+b),Z)−H(a(1−b),Z)]2ab.\widehat G_b=\frac{D[H(a(1+b),Z)-H(a(1-b),Z)]}{2ab}. Average across independent draws. The same ZZ in both terms reduces variance; this does not remove finite-difference bias. The call’s pathwise sample contributions are GΔPW=D𝟏{ST>K}ST/S0,G_{\Delta}^{PW}=D\mathbf1_{\{S_T>K\}}S_T/S_0, G𝒱PW=D𝟏{ST>K}ST(−σT+TZ).G_{\mathcal V}^{PW}=D\mathbf1_{\{S_T>K\}}S_T(-\sigma T+\sqrt T Z). Differentiation under the expectation is justified for the continuous call under these assumptions. The digital’s individual derivative is zero almost everywhere, although its price derivative need not vanish. It is not a valid estimator here.

For likelihood-ratio estimation, differentiate the lognormal density of the observed price holding it fixed. The scores are sS0=ZS0σT,sσ=Z2−1σ−ZT.s_{S_0}=\frac{Z}{S_0\sigma\sqrt T},\qquad s_\sigma=\frac{Z^2-1}{\sigma}-Z\sqrt T. Multiply each score by DHDH and take its sample mean. Both digital and call payoffs are admissible. A likelihood-ratio estimator may be noisy even if unbiased.

3. Arithmetic Asian sensitivities

Let 0=t0<t1<⋯<tm=T0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_m=T, δtj=tj−tj−1\delta t_j=t_j-t_{j-1} and Zj=(Wtj−Wtj−1)/δtjZ_j=(W_{t_j}-W_{t_{j-1}})/\sqrt{\delta t_j}. The joint density factors into lognormal conditional transition densities. Holding all fixing levels fixed, only the first transition depends directly on S0S_0; every transition contributes to the volatility score: sS0=Z1S0σt1,sσ=∑j=1m[Zj2−1σ−Zjδtj].s_{S_0}=\frac{Z_1}{S_0\sigma\sqrt{t_1}},\qquad s_\sigma=\sum_{j=1}^m\left[\frac{Z_j^2-1}{\sigma}-Z_j\sqrt{\delta t_j}\right]. For A=m−1∑jStjA=m^{-1}\sum_j S_{t_j}, the pathwise samples are GΔPW=D𝟏{A>K}A/S0,G_{\Delta}^{PW}=D\mathbf1_{\{A>K\}}A/S_0, G𝒱PW=D𝟏{A>K}m∑j=1mStj(Wtj−σtj).G_{\mathcal V}^{PW}=\frac{D\mathbf1_{\{A>K\}}}{m}\sum_{j=1}^m S_{t_j}(W_{t_j}-\sigma t_j). The arithmetic payoff kink matters: on a fixed sample, a bump can move paths across the strike. Refine the bump and count crossings; equality with the pathwise derivative for sufficiently small bumps is a diagnostic, not a proof of small population bias. The geometric average admits an analytic lognormal price and may be used as a control.

4. Error, uncertainty and cost

For LL independent replications Ĝℓ\widehat G_\ell and reference G*G^*, report mean error and RMSE=L−1∑ℓ(Ĝℓ−G*)2\mathrm{RMSE}=\sqrt{L^{-1}\sum_\ell(\widehat G_\ell-G^*)^2}. For an Asian numerical reference, report its own uncertainty separately; differences smaller than this uncertainty cannot establish a ranking. Time independent replications with the same hardware and exclude unrelated setup. Record both RMSE and elapsed time. Confidence intervals must reflect independent draws or replications, not treat paired antithetic samples as independent.
