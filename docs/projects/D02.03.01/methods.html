Arbitrage-Free Interpolation of Implied Volatility Smiles

Which interpolation method balances fidelity to sparse and noisy quotes, accuracy at unobserved strikes and the absence of butterfly arbitrage? What is the cost of imposing no-arbitrage restrictions?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Finite-grid no arbitrage

For fixed expiry, forward FF and discount DD, a call must satisfy D(F−K)+≤C(K)≤DFD(F-K)^+\le C(K)\le DF. On ordered strikes K1<⋯<KnK_1<\cdots<K_n, define secants sj=[C(Kj+1)−C(Kj)]/(Kj+1−Kj)s_j=[C(K_{j+1})-C(K_j)]/(K_{j+1}-K_j). Required slopes are in [−D,0][-D,0] and nondecreasing. The convexity inequality corresponds to a nonnegative cost for a properly weighted butterfly on irregular strikes. In a smooth model CKK=DfQ(K)C_{KK}=D f_Q(K); a piecewise-linear interpolant instead has discrete atoms at changes in slope. Do not treat its knots as a smooth density.

Projection versus interpolation

Compare cubic IV interpolation, unconstrained price interpolation, and a constrained price projection minimizing ∑j∈𝒯aj(cj−Cjobs)2\sum_{j\in\mathcal T}a_j(c_j-C_j^{obs})^2 under the above linear inequalities with prespecified positive weights aja_j. Interpolate projected prices linearly on the interior grid. Test all knot intervals and evaluate errors on dense untouched clean strikes. Record optimization failures, actual adjustments to quotes, and size of butterfly violations before and after projection. Individual price clipping does not guarantee convexity. Endpoints and extrapolation require separate assumptions; the baseline is an interior-domain exercise.

Research checks

Use irregular strike spacing, two independently constructed clean laws and fixed noise draws. Report adjustment cost and holdout recovery separately; distinguish discrete state-price mass from a differentiable density.
