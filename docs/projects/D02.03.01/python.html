D02.03.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

An implied-volatility smile can appear smooth while implying a negative butterfly price. With sparse noisy quotes, forcing price convexity changes the fit, and the trade-off is measurable against an independently generated clean surface. This project focuses on a single expiry and on the economic meaning of finite-grid restrictions, not on global extrapolation.

Research question: Which interpolation method balances fidelity to sparse and noisy quotes, accuracy at unobserved strikes and the absence of butterfly arbitrage? What is the cost of imposing no-arbitrage restrictions?

Student-owned research

Use irregular strike spacing, two independently constructed clean laws and fixed noise draws. Report adjustment cost and holdout recovery separately; distinguish discrete state-price mass from a differentiable density.

  1. Derive arbitrage inequalities and construct a deliberately violating three-strike butterfly example.

  2. Fit cubic interpolation in IV space (after price inversion), unconstrained price interpolation and constrained price projection plus piecewise-linear interpolation. Use nonuniform finite differences; record infeasible inversions and optimizer status.

  3. Evaluate dense held-out clean call prices, slopes, convexity violations and magnitude of negative butterfly costs.

  4. Quantify adjustment to observed noisy quotes and error trade-off for different sparsity and noise levels; inspect reconstructed interval state-price mass rather than differentiating kinks as a smooth density.

Minimum viable thesis: Correct finite-grid arbitrage restrictions, three interpolation alternatives, two clean DGPs, controlled noise experiment, held-out errors and economic interpretation of convexity. No requirement for a smooth global density.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
