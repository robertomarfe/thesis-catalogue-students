Exchange Rate Jumps and Currency Option Smiles

Compensated jump mixture

Let J∼N(mJ,sJ2)J\sim N(m_J,s_J^2) and NT∼Poisson(λT)N_T\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda T) be independent of the diffusion. The compensator is ζ=emJ+sJ2/2−1\zeta=e^{m_J+s_J^2/2}-1, making Eℚd[ST]=F=S0e(rd−rf)TE^{\mathbb Q_d}[S_T]=F=S_0e^{(r_d-r_f)T}. Given NT=nN_T=n, the log-forward ratio is Gaussian with mean −σ2T/2−λTζ+nmJ-\sigma^2T/2-\lambda T\zeta+nm_J and variance σ2T+nsJ2\sigma^2T+ns_J^2. Weight conditional lognormal payoff values by Poisson probabilities and discount domestically. For call-tail control, monitor omitted probability and omitted stock-weighted mass (the latter corresponds to Poisson mean λTE[eJ]\lambda T E[e^J]).

Match and compare

Solve the nonnegative diffusion volatility at the fixed ATM target; if jump-only price exceeds target, record infeasibility. Evaluate the same K/FK/F and maturity, invert domestic option prices with the same rates, and report skew/curvature, truncation and Monte Carlo uncertainty. For inverted FX quotes, the pricing measure changes: do not invert the process under unchanged domestic Q. Physical jump estimates alone do not identify risk-neutral jumps.

Research checks

Check martingale, parity, zero intensity, truncation including stock-weighted tail, ATM feasibility and independent Monte Carlo. Report infeasible cases; no observed smile claim.
