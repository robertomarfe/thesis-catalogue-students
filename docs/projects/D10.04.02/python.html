D10.04.02 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

FX jump risk changes the relative prices of calls and puts in the wings. Holding the forward and the ATM option price fixed provides a controlled comparison of smile asymmetry and curvature instead of confounding jump effects with the overall volatility level.

Research question: Holding the forward and ATM option price fixed, how do jump frequency, direction and size affect FX smile slope and wing prices?

Student-owned research

Check martingale, parity, zero intensity, truncation including stock-weighted tail, ATM feasibility and independent Monte Carlo. Report infeasible cases; no observed smile claim.

  1. Derive compensator and conditional moments; check E_Q[S_T]=F and parity.

  2. Implement mixture and MC reference, verify zero-intensity limit.

  3. Solve ATM price match, compare skew, butterfly and wing implied vol in fixed log-forward moneyness.

  4. Vary jump frequency and severity at approximately fixed jump variance; assess observational equivalence and failure of single-slice identification.

  5. Reverse quote direction only using the correct foreign measure/numeraire, or keep original orientation throughout; document interpretation.

Minimum viable thesis: Domestic-Q Merton mixture, matched ATM scenarios, skew/curvature comparisons and independent MC check. Distinct from equity jump pricing and diffusion FX pricing D10.04.01.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
