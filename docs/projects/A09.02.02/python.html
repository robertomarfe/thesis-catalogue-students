Goal

Currency carry earns a payoff by holding currencies with higher interest-rate-related carry against lower carry currencies. Its exposure to global risk-off states may be important even if its unconditional average payoff is positive. Distinguish a contemporaneous risk-state description from a forecast made before the return period.

Research question: Are currency carry returns lower and market exposures larger in risk-off states, and can lagged global risk measures forecast carry returns?

Student-owned research

Audit the G10 gross definition and dates before use. Report state counts, training thresholds, information lags and common forecast origins. Do not substitute other carry factors or claim causal effects.

  1. Document published portfolio construction, funding and gross/net conventions.

  2. Compare average return, volatility, skewness, drawdown and losses by contemporaneous market/VIX state.

  3. Estimate carry on market and market*stress interaction, with HAC and stress-count reporting.

  4. Forecast next-month carry using lagged VIX/NFCI against expanding mean; initial 120 months, no full-sample thresholds.

  5. Compare gross vs net, fixed subperiods and paired block-bootstrap uncertainty.

Minimum viable thesis: One documented G10 gross carry series, common monthly market/VIX data, state-dependent returns and lagged forecasting; net optional if accessible. Distinct from A09.02.01 bilateral forward premium puzzle.
