Limits to Arbitrage and the Persistence of Mispricing

Research question

How do wealth or position constraints and noise persistence affect the magnitude and persistence of mispricing in a stylized finite-horizon market?

Mathematical framework

Let d=P−Fd=P-F, noise demand n−βdn-\beta d, arbitrage x=clip(−d/a,−K,K)x=\operatorname{clip}(-d/a,-K,K); clearing is n−βd+x=0n-\beta d+x=0.

Interior d=n/(β+1/a)d=n/(\beta+1/a), binding d=(n+x)/βd=(n+x)/\beta. Verify continuity, independent scalar root and cap limits. Liquidation at known F is imposed. Wealth changes by position times price change, not a preference penalty unless explicitly charged. Gaussian noise may give nonpositive prices; report all invalid rates and label conditional results.

Research checks

Verify analytical regime boundaries, continuity and clearing with independent scalar root solution; verify trading gains and wealth identities including cap=0/infinity. Use matched noise and report binding rates, loss/negative wealth and invalid price rates for every cell. Full 50k-path experiment and endogenous wealth constraint remain student work.
