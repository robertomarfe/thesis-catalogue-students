# Research plan | A01.01.02

## Study design and independent work

Freeze provider labels, estimate realized beta separately, compare all within-size low/high groups and refit in paired month blocks. BAB is complementary only; do not rename an unscaled spread BAB or infer a funding constraint from the line alone.

## Required tasks

1. Plot mean returns and realized betas for beta sorts and within-size groups; compare CAPM-predicted line and fitted slope.

2. Estimate CAPM and FF3/FF5 alphas with HAC intervals; report low-minus-high raw return and beta-adjusted alpha separately.

3. Re-estimate exposures and means in paired date-block bootstrap, including uncertainty in cross-sectional slope.

4. Evaluate BAB mean, volatility, market exposure and factor alpha over its own common sample.

5. Compare weighting schemes and subperiods, keeping all beta portfolios in reported tables.

## Required exhibits

Security market line plots, beta and alpha tables, within-size comparisons, BAB performance and exposure table, joint uncertainty and subperiod diagnostics.

## Robustness

Use preformed portfolio definitions, not full-sample beta sorting of stocks. A flat line is descriptive, not evidence that funding constraints cause it. Published BAB is not identical to an unscaled low-high spread; factor controls are not necessarily causal. A12.01.02 studies funding conditions rather than the security market line.

## Minimum viable thesis

Beta-sorted and size-beta portfolios, CAPM line and alpha comparison, joint uncertainty, FF controls and independent BAB evidence if source access permits.

## Optional extension

Test regional BAB series with common USD factors and distinguish cross-market differences from local sample composition.

## Prerequisites

CAPM, multifactor models, regression, portfolio data and Python.

## Thesis outline

1. CAPM and the low-beta anomaly;

2. Beta-sorted portfolios and BAB;

3. Data and inference;

4. Security market line and factor controls;

5. Robustness and interpretation.

## Milestones

W1-3: CAPM and BAB theory; W4-6: portfolio data and beta estimates; W7-9: pricing tests; W10-12: joint inference and BAB; W13-16: robustness and writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
