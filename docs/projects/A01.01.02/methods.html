The Low-Beta Anomaly and the Risk-Return Trade-Off

Is the security market line flatter than the CAPM prediction across publicly available beta-sorted portfolios, and is the low-beta evidence robust to size and alternative factor controls?

Sorting versus realized exposure

The formation signal is determined by the provider before the holding period. Estimate realized β̂i\widehat\beta_i on common months from xit=αi+βixMt+ϵitx_{it}=\alpha_i+\beta_ix_{Mt}+\epsilon_{it}. A descriptive slope from x‾i=a+bβ̂i+ui\bar x_i=a+b\widehat\beta_i+u_i is affected by beta estimation error and dispersion. Report intercept and slope jointly, and compare to (0,x‾M)(0,\bar x_M).

Different long-short payoffs

For illustrative positive ex ante betas βL,βH\beta_L,\beta_H, a beta-matched excess-payoff construction is xL/βL−xH/βHx_L/\beta_L-x_H/\beta_H. The cash and leverage conventions matter; this stylized expression is not a substitute for the provider’s BAB methodology. Contrast it with the unscaled xL−xHx_L-x_H. FF controls test model-dependent alphas, not a causal funding mechanism.

Research checks

Freeze provider labels, estimate realized beta separately, compare all within-size low/high groups and refit in paired month blocks. BAB is complementary only; do not rename an unscaled spread BAB or infer a funding constraint from the line alone.
