Foreign accrual

Under domestic pricing probabilities dS/S=(rd−rf)dt+σdWℚddS/S=(r_d-r_f)dt+\sigma dW^{\mathbb Q_d}. At time tt, a foreign money-market unit has domestic value SterftS_t e^{r_ft}, so a position replicating spot delta holds Δte−rft\Delta_t e^{-r_ft} units. Domestic cash accrues at rdr_d; account for foreign accrual and self-financing at each rebalance. Optional spot/rate proxies do not provide observed market option prices.
