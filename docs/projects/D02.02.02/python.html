Goal

Calibration results from actual option quotes depend on the economic model and on how the quotes are selected, aligned and weighted. A study of market prices must separate market microstructure and measurement choices from model differences. This proposal compares slice-based and structural models only after a genuinely usable, authorized quote panel has been established.

Research question: How sensitive are SVI and Heston calibration and interpolation to quote cleaning, error weighting and held-out market prices on the same underlying?

  1. Audit license, schema, quote coverage and forward/parity consistency before accepting thesis.

  2. Implement BSM IV and calibrated synthetic benchmark, compare Heston and SVI.

  3. Fit on alternating eligible strikes with fixed date-wise splits, hold out strikes and one maturity only for joint Heston (SVI interpolation requires an explicit maturity rule).

  4. Compare midpoint and spread-weighted loss, fraction inside spreads, price/IV error and arbitrage-grid diagnostics.

  5. Assess sensitivity to cleaning, multistart and date/maturity selection.

Minimum viable thesis: Permission and coverage gate, 10+ suitable dates with 3 expiries, BSM plus SVI/Heston comparison on common held-out strikes, auditable cleaning and uncertainty.
