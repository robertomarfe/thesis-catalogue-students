D07.03.02 · Derivatives · Fixed income & interest rates

Swaption Pricing and the Impact of Yield Curve Volatility

How do volatility level and mean reversion affect European payer swaption prices and hedge sensitivities across expiry and tenor in a one-factor Hull–White model fitted to consistent initial yield curves?

Synthetic core · Level 3/3 · Research draft

Synthetic core. No external dataset required for the core design; optional extensions may require data.

Input contract

Synthetic flat 2% and upward zero curves, annual fixed payments, option expiries 1,2,5 years, swap tenors 2,5,10 years; ATM and +/-50 bp strikes (keep K>=0 for decomposition). GSW curves optional illustration https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm, not swaption market quotes.
