Swaption Pricing and the Impact of Yield Curve Volatility

Research question

How do volatility level and mean reversion affect European payer swaption prices and hedge sensitivities across expiry and tenor in a one-factor Hull–White model fitted to consistent initial yield curves?

For exercise uu, let Iu=∫0uxsdsI_u=\int_0^u x_sds. The pair (xu,Iu)(x_u,I_u) is centered Gaussian with

A convenient conditional bond formula is

3. Jamshidian decomposition and independent check

Set ki=P(u,Ti;x*)k_i=P(u,T_i;x^*). Since all bond prices fall together with the factor,

(1−∑iciP(u,Ti))+=∑ici[ki−P(u,Ti)]+.(1-\sum_i c_iP(u,T_i))^+=\sum_i c_i[k_i-P(u,T_i)]^+.

Each term is a bond put. Under the uu-forward measure its lognormal standard deviation is Ba(Ti−u)vxB_a(T_i-u)\sqrt{v_x}; discount by P(0,u)P(0,u). The swaption_hw routine uses this decomposition.
