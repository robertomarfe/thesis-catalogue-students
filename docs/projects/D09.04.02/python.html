Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Standalone exposure and default probabilities are insufficient to determine a counterparty loss when the two risks depend on one another. Isolate the contribution of a specified joint distribution at fixed marginals, rather than changing both the intensity level and dependence.

Research question: How much can wrong-way dependence alter unilateral CVA when the standalone default probabilities and exposure dynamics are held fixed?

Student-owned research

Check marginal survival at all grid dates, exposure distributions, paired loss differences and grid refinement; distinguish dependence from changes in hazard or recovery. Contrast long and short forwards. A common latent horizon across maturities is a possible redesign, not part of the current frozen scope.

  1. Validate clean forward and marginal default survival against exp(-lambda t).

  2. Reproduce independent CVA from analytic lognormal EPE and default increments.

  3. Estimate paired CVA change under the copula with unchanged marginals and uncertainty intervals.

  4. Study exposure at default, maturity and rare-default Monte Carlo error.

  5. Decompose change from dependence versus separate hazard/recovery sensitivity and contrast long/short forward.

Minimum viable thesis: Independent-CVA benchmark and fixed-marginal dependence sensitivity for one forward, with sign convention, marginal checks and confidence intervals.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
