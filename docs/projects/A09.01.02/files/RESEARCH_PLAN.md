# Research plan | A09.01.02

## Study design and independent work

Verify the four disjoint constituents and common USD RF. Exclude the overlapping Developed index from portfolio holdings. Record the rolling/regime timing, variance decomposition and sample dependence.

## Required tasks

1. Verify regional definitions and disjoint coverage; construct synchronized USD returns.

2. Estimate full and rolling covariance/correlation, global and leave-one-out betas, and PCA variance shares with standardized returns.

3. Compare equally weighted portfolio volatility with average stand-alone variance and evaluate drawdowns and turnover.

4. Compare high/low lagged volatility regimes using common-date block intervals; report crisis episodes as descriptive.

5. Repeat with 36/120-month windows and subperiods, distinguish changes in volatility from changes in correlation.

## Required exhibits

Regional data dictionary, rolling correlation and beta plots, PCA diagnostics, diversification and regime tables with uncertainty and portfolio accounting.

## Robustness

Global index overlaps regional assets; leave-one-out benchmark is an equal-weight proxy, not a market-cap global index. USD returns mix currency and local equity risk. Do not equate correlation with market integration, or include overlapping Developed alongside its regions in the portfolio.

## Minimum viable thesis

Four disjoint regions, common monthly sample, rolling comovement measures, equal-weight diversification benchmark and regime comparison.

## Optional extension

Use documented local-currency or emerging-market return series to study currency contributions and developed/emerging diversification separately.

## Prerequisites

Portfolio theory, factor regressions, PCA, time-series inference and Python.

## Thesis outline

1. International asset pricing and diversification;

2. Covariance and factor exposures;

3. Regional data design;

4. Time variation and regime evidence;

5. Portfolio and integration implications.

## Milestones

W1-3: diversification theory and region definitions; W4-6: panel and return construction; W7-9: rolling factor/covariance analysis; W10-12: portfolio and regime results; W13-16: robustness and writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
