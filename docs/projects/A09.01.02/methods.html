Regional co-movement and portfolio risk

Let Rt∈ℝ4R_t\in\mathbb R^4 collect regional USD total returns, not market excess returns. For fixed monthly weights, w⊤Σw=∑iwi2σi2+2∑i<jwiwjσiσjρij.w^\top\Sigma w=\sum_iw_i^2\sigma_i^2+2\sum_{i<j}w_iw_j\sigma_i\sigma_j\rho_{ij}. The first term is within-region variance contribution, the second between-region contribution. Rolling correlations, common-factor betas and PCA on covariance or correlation answer distinct questions; fix scaling and explain changes in eigenvectors. Use a common 60-month window, and label the earliest date available for any statistic.

Implementable regime comparison

For equal weights the period portfolio return is w⊤Rtw^\top R_t with monthly rebalancing. Compound total returns, not excess returns. Define high volatility from information available before the period using a training-only threshold; contemporaneous crisis splits are descriptive. Use the same calendar, report regime counts and paired block intervals. A leave-one-out equal-weight regional reference is not a market-cap weighted Developed index. USD returns combine local equity and FX risk; correlation alone is not market integration.
