Goal

Observed long yields combine expected future short rates with compensation for risk, but the two components are not separately traded observables. Published model-based decompositions enable a disciplined descriptive investigation of which macro-financial states are associated with estimated term premia.

Research question: Which observable macro-financial states are associated with the level and slope of published Treasury term-premium estimates, and how sensitive is the interpretation to the term-structure model?

Student-owned research

Document exact units, maturity, release and decomposition convention. Do not interpret coefficients as causal effects or real-time forecasts. Keep model comparison conditional on verified coverage.

  1. Validate ACM decomposition identity from actual file, units, date and maturity columns; compare fitted yields with GSW.

  2. Plot components by maturity and summarize level and slope across episodes.

  3. Regress TP and EH separately on standardized lagged macro states, use HAC and paired blocks.

  4. Compare explanatory contribution and stability of associations across maturities and pre/post-2008 or fixed subsamples.

  5. Discuss generated-regressor and model risk; compare a second published model if gate cleared, not a replication of proprietary inputs.

Minimum viable thesis: ACM components with verified identity, two maturities and two macro predictors, descriptive regression with uncertainty and model limitations.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
