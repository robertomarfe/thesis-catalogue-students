Understanding the Economic Determinants of the Bond Term Premium

Which observable macro-financial states are associated with the level and slope of published Treasury term-premium estimates, and how sensitive is the interpretation to the term-structure model?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Estimated objects

The expectations component is the model-specific average expected short rate, possibly with a separately defined convexity adjustment. Use the exact provider decomposition rather than mechanically subtracting one observed yield from another. Check yfit−EH−TPy^{\mathrm{fit}}-EH-TP within published rounding; distinguish fitted from observed curve yields.

Descriptive regressions

Regress TPt(n)TP_t(n) and TPt(10)−TPt(2)TP_t(10)-TP_t(2) on the predetermined macro states. Use lagged series for information timing, while acknowledging revised macro data and full-sample estimates of generated premia. Report the analogous associations for EHEH and fitted yield and the residual structure. HAC or time-block intervals do not remove model-generated-regressor uncertainty. Cross-model agreement is a robustness check, not identification of the true premium.

Research checks

Document exact units, maturity, release and decomposition convention. Do not interpret coefficients as causal effects or real-time forecasts. Keep model comparison conditional on verified coverage.
