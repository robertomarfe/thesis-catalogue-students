## Motivation

Observed long yields combine expected future short rates with compensation for risk, but the two components are not separately traded observables. Published model-based decompositions enable a disciplined descriptive investigation of which macro-financial states are associated with estimated term premia.

## Research question

Which observable macro-financial states are associated with the level and slope of published Treasury term-premium estimates, and how sensitive is the interpretation to the term-structure model?

## Economic mechanism

In a compatible published decomposition, $y_t^{\mathrm{fit}}(n)=EH_t(n)+TP_t(n)$.

## Research design

Use the documented ACM components, common maturities and lagged macro-financial predictors. Verify provider identities before running descriptive regressions; compare variation of fitted yield, expected-rate component and term premium. A second model is an extension only after conventions and coverage match.

Document exact units, maturity, release and decomposition convention. Do not interpret coefficients as causal effects or real-time forecasts. Keep model comparison conditional on verified coverage.

## Required exhibits

Yield decomposition plots, maturity-state summary, coefficient/partial R2 table, component and subsample sensitivity.

## Minimum viable thesis

ACM components with verified identity, two maturities and two macro predictors, descriptive regression with uncertainty and model limitations.

## Scope and feasibility

Published ACM series uses estimated parameters and may incorporate full-sample information; lagging predictors does not make historical estimates real-time. No causal decomposition of risk sources or actual traded premium. Do not subtract survey expectations of a different horizon as if exact EH.

**Prerequisites:** Term-structure models, macro finance, regression, time-series inference.

**Optional extension:** Compare ACM with Board three-factor estimates after reconciling dates, compounding and model definitions; use same sample, no claim of model truth.
