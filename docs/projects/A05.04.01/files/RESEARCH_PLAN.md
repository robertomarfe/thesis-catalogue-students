# Research plan | A05.04.01

## Study design and independent work

Document exact units, maturity, release and decomposition convention. Do not interpret coefficients as causal effects or real-time forecasts. Keep model comparison conditional on verified coverage.

## Required tasks

1. Validate ACM decomposition identity from actual file, units, date and maturity columns; compare fitted yields with GSW.

2. Plot components by maturity and summarize level and slope across episodes.

3. Regress TP and EH separately on standardized lagged macro states, use HAC and paired blocks.

4. Compare explanatory contribution and stability of associations across maturities and pre/post-2008 or fixed subsamples.

5. Discuss generated-regressor and model risk; compare a second published model if gate cleared, not a replication of proprietary inputs.

## Required exhibits

Yield decomposition plots, maturity-state summary, coefficient/partial R2 table, component and subsample sensitivity.

## Robustness

Published ACM series uses estimated parameters and may incorporate full-sample information; lagging predictors does not make historical estimates real-time. No causal decomposition of risk sources or actual traded premium. Do not subtract survey expectations of a different horizon as if exact EH.

## Minimum viable thesis

ACM components with verified identity, two maturities and two macro predictors, descriptive regression with uncertainty and model limitations.

## Optional extension

Compare ACM with Board three-factor estimates after reconciling dates, compounding and model definitions; use same sample, no claim of model truth.

## Prerequisites

Term-structure models, macro finance, regression, time-series inference.

## Thesis outline

1. Expectations and term premia;

2. Model-based decomposition;

3. Data and macro states;

4. Conditional evidence;

5. Model uncertainty.

## Milestones

W1-3: model definitions and acquisition; W4-6: identity and matching; W7-9: descriptive regressions; W10-12: model/episode robustness; W13-16: dissertation.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
