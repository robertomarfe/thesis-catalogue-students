Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Stochastic discounting makes the choice of pricing measure economically meaningful but not a source of pricing disagreement. A change of numeraire reallocates probability weights to make a different traded unit the reference asset. Verify the equivalence locally, including self-financing replication.

Research question: How can one verify that money-market and forward-measure valuation give identical prices even when interest rates are stochastic?

Check two invented probability weights sum to one. This artificial example does not validate the full research design or actual data.

Student-owned research

Check all three measure transformations at every node, local payoff rank, transition sums, price equality and replication residuals. Extend only after the two-period benchmark is independently reproduced.

  1. Verify discounted stock and bond martingales under Q^B at every node.

  2. Derive local and full-path forward-measure probabilities and check sums and positivity.

  3. Price claims by backward induction under B and T-bond and compare node-by-node, including replication where one-step payoffs span the binary branches.

  4. Repeat using stock numeraire and test sensitivity to rate/stock dependence by changing terminal payoff tree or r states.

  5. Deliberately omit the payoff-unit conversion or density and quantify the resulting pricing inconsistency as a diagnostic.

Minimum viable thesis: Complete two-period example, money-market/forward/stock measure transformations, at least three claim types, nodewise pricing equality and replication residuals below numerical tolerance.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
