The Risk-Free Rate Puzzle under Time-Additive Utility

Can time-additive power utility with plausible risk aversion and discounting reproduce both the mean and time variation of real short rates given observed US consumption growth?

For |ϕ|<1|\phi|<1, the stationary standard deviation of the annualized quarterly log safe rate is 4γ|ϕ|σ/1−ϕ24\gamma|\phi|\sigma/\sqrt{1-\phi^2}. The precautionary term lowers the mean log safe rate. In the iid limit the model conditional rate is constant; realized real nominal bills can nevertheless fluctuate with inflation. Solve beta from the mean rate, retaining inadmissible values; do not treat ex post quarterly bills as a real zero yield. Refit the conditional growth model in joint calendar-aware blocks.

Research checks
