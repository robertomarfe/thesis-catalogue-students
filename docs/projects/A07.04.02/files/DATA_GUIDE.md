## Construction and timing
Use original 20-year initial window; rolling 5% and 10% market quantile estimated before each crash classification, with counts. For each exposure window estimate ordinary beta and crash interaction (include state intercept as robustness) or conditional residual mean. Freeze exposure estimates at t before next-month returns and sorting. Long-short factors descriptive; 25 Size/BM and Size/Momentum panels separately, industries alternative. Report few-crash instability and noncrash placebo, no causal interpretation.
