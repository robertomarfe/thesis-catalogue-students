Are Equity Factor Premia Compensation for Crash Risk?

Do factor strategies with worse payoffs in market crashes earn higher average excess returns, and is the relation robust to ordinary market beta and sampling uncertainty?

Construction and timing

Use original 20-year initial window; rolling 5% and 10% market quantile estimated before each crash classification, with counts. For each exposure window estimate ordinary beta and crash interaction (include state intercept as robustness) or conditional residual mean. Freeze exposure estimates at t before next-month returns and sorting. Long-short factors descriptive; 25 Size/BM and Size/Momentum panels separately, industries alternative. Report few-crash instability and noncrash placebo, no causal interpretation.

Acceptance and first meeting

Verify rolling threshold calculated on prior window and unchanged by future observations. Synthetic interaction regression with known coefficients; refuse states with insufficient support.

Build a local inventory, inspect each actual schema and license, save an input ledger, row counts and actual dates. Validate session calendar, coverage, duplicate keys and missingness before merging; do not compress gaps. Provide joint sample and training/validation/evaluation counts, mature-label counts for forecasts and state/event counts for conditional estimates. No real inputs bundled or verified.
