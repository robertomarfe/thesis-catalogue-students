Data: Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Anomaly returns may attenuate after the discovery sample or after publication. These are different comparisons. Publication dates are not randomly assigned, and a reconstructed return history is not a record of what investors knew in real time.

Research question

How do anomaly returns and factor-adjusted alphas change across original-sample, post-sample pre-publication and post-publication windows in a fixed set of documented predictors?

Economic mechanism

With predictor fixed effects, Lit=ai+bsIitpostsample+bpIitpostpublication+uitL_{it}=a_i+b_sI_{it}^{post\ sample}+b_pI_{it}^{post\ publication}+u_{it}, where the indicators are cumulative.

Research design

Verify original sample ends and publication dates for the original 20–40 predictor design using one OSAP revision. Freeze ordered windows, signs and availability rules. Contrast same-predictor changes in raw and benchmark-adjusted returns, using fixed-window sensitivity and calendar-dependence inference.

Retain the minimum 20 dated predictors and original minimum coverage; distinguish a permitted two-window subset from a three-window design. Log every date source and exclusion; do not equate journal publication with first dissemination or the OSAP migration with verified access.

Required exhibits

Date registry with original sources; sample attrition table; paired mean/alpha changes; event-time plots and uncertainty.

Minimum viable thesis

At least 20 dated predictors, three-window or clearly labeled two-window subset, paired aggregate inference and documented publication timing.

Scope and feasibility

No invented sample dates; avoid double-counting renamed signals, unbalanced composition or comparing disjoint anomaly sets. OSAP returns are reconstructed, not historical real-time published portfolios; publication effect is descriptive association.

Prerequisites: Panel econometrics, factor pricing, data cleaning, Python.

Optional extension: Compare decay across independently coded liquidity or implementation difficulty groups; include working-paper release dates if documented.

Milestones

W1-3: literature and dates; W4-6: OSAP merge and eligibility; W7-9: panel analysis; W10-12: robustness; W13-16: write and archive.
