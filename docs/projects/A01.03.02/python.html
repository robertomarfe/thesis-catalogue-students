A01.03.02 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage.

Goal

Anomaly returns may attenuate after the discovery sample or after publication. These are different comparisons. Publication dates are not randomly assigned, and a reconstructed return history is not a record of what investors knew in real time.

Research question: How do anomaly returns and factor-adjusted alphas change across original-sample, post-sample pre-publication and post-publication windows in a fixed set of documented predictors?

Student-owned research

Retain the minimum 20 dated predictors and original minimum coverage; distinguish a permitted two-window subset from a three-window design. Log every date source and exclusion; do not equate journal publication with first dissemination or the OSAP migration with verified access.

  1. Build dated provenance table and document exclusions; freeze signed return series.

  2. Plot anomaly-level mean returns in the three periods, equal-weighted across signals.

  3. Estimate panel with anomaly effects, then calendar effects on common-support sample.

  4. Compare raw and factor-adjusted differences; common time block bootstrap with anomaly sensitivity and multiple-test adjustment for individual signals.

  5. Test fixed windows, publication-date lags and placebo dates chosen ex ante; distinguish short pre-publication periods.

Minimum viable thesis: At least 20 dated predictors, three-window or clearly labeled two-window subset, paired aggregate inference and documented publication timing.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
