Current review draft; approval pending. Empirical design blocked pending authorized access and coverage; not assignable on this design.

Goal

Corporate quality spreads often widen in deteriorating economic conditions, but rating composition, maturity and liquidity can also move them. The study is descriptive: compare the Baa-Aaa yield difference across the business cycle without treating it as a pure default-risk measure.

Research question: How do the level and changes of the Baa-Aaa quality spread relate to economic activity and financial uncertainty, and are these associations stable across recessions?

  1. Plot quality spread, growth and uncertainty; compute distributions and recession/nonrecession differences with date-block confidence intervals.

  2. Estimate autoregressive benchmark and regressions with lagged spread, growth and VIX; use both levels and changes, discuss persistence.

  3. Report standardized marginal associations and incremental explanatory power on a common sample; examine lead-lag correlations as descriptive.

  4. Repeat excluding the 2008-09 and 2020 episodes and using annual growth versus monthly growth.

  5. Compare fitted and realized changes in held-out episodes without tuning on them.

Minimum viable thesis: Monthly matched panel, transparent Baa-Aaa construction, recession comparison, autoregressive and two-variable macro regressions, HAC and crisis sensitivity checks.
