Measurement and alignment

For seasonally adjusted industrial production ItI_t, annual growth in log-percent is gt=100log(It/It−12)g_t=100\log(I_t/I_{t-12}). Average daily VIX closes within each month before merging, retaining month-level observation counts. Match the exact monthly calendar; missing months are not zero and are not adjacent observations. Baa-Aaa is an average rating-quality spread, not a maturity-matched traded credit portfolio.

Conditional associations

A descriptive specification is st=a+ϕst−1+bgt−1+cVIXt−1+ut.s_t=a+\phi s_{t-1}+b g_{t-1}+c\,\mathrm{VIX}_{t-1}+u_t. Compare the autoregressive benchmark and a specification with Δst\Delta s_t as target on a common sample. Report standardized effects, persistence and HAC or block uncertainty. Recession chronology and revised industrial production are retrospective; crisis/subperiod selection must be predeclared. Association does not identify causal effects or a physical default probability.
