Corporate Credit Quality Spreads and Macroeconomic Conditions

Research plan | D09.03.01

Study design and independent work

This design remains blocked until the supervisor verifies rights for both Moody series and actual input coverage. Record original filename, vintage and permitted retention separately for each series. No substitute index or simulation can satisfy the unchanged empirical MVT.

Required tasks

  1. Plot quality spread, growth and uncertainty; compute distributions and recession/nonrecession differences with date-block confidence intervals.

  2. Estimate autoregressive benchmark and regressions with lagged spread, growth and VIX; use both levels and changes, discuss persistence.

  3. Report standardized marginal associations and incremental explanatory power on a common sample; examine lead-lag correlations as descriptive.

  4. Repeat excluding the 2008-09 and 2020 episodes and using annual growth versus monthly growth.

  5. Compare fitted and realized changes in held-out episodes without tuning on them.

Required exhibits

Data dictionary, spread/activity time-series figure, conditional means, regression tables with HAC intervals, subsample and episode sensitivity plots.

Robustness

Check maturity and rating-composition caveats, source terms, units, missing values and lag choice. Spread is not a CDS premium or a pure default-risk measure. Avoid interpreting contemporaneous macro associations as causal or tradable forecasts; revised activity data are retrospective.

Minimum viable thesis

Monthly matched panel, transparent Baa-Aaa construction, recession comparison, autoregressive and two-variable macro regressions, HAC and crisis sensitivity checks.

Optional extension

Compare with a separately licensed corporate OAS dataset or evaluate pseudo-out-of-sample prediction using dated macro vintages.

Prerequisites

Fixed income, regression, time-series diagnostics, Python or R; no individual-bond data required.

Thesis outline

  1. Corporate bond yields and credit quality;

  2. Credit spread mechanisms and literature;

  3. Data and identification limits;

  4. Descriptive and econometric evidence;

  5. Stability and interpretation.

Milestones

W1-3: theory, source terms and variable definitions; W4-6: acquisition and descriptive analysis; W7-9: benchmark and macro regressions; W10-12: persistence and crisis diagnostics; W13-16: interpretation, reproducibility and writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
