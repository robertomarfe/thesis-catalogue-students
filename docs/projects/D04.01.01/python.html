Student-owned research

  1. Derive BSM hedge and self-financing cash update; verify one deterministic path by hand and test accounting identities. 2. Simulate exact GBM with nested rebalance grids; compare discounted terminal error mean, SD, RMSE and quantiles. 3. Plot error convergence on log-log axes against theoretical order under regularity; avoid assuming a finite-grid exact exponent. 4. Analyze error by strike, maturity, terminal moneyness and initial gamma; conditional results on terminal outcomes are descriptive only. 5. Report stock turnover and optionally exposure through time, and use independent seeds for Monte Carlo uncertainty.

Minimum viable thesis: Exact GBM, verified self-financing hedge, four frequencies and three strikes, quantitative convergence and distribution analysis with Monte Carlo precision.
