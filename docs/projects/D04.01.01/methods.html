D04.01.01 · Synthetic diagnostic · Companion to the research proposal

1. Pricing and simulation are different operations

Under ℙ\mathbb P, the exact stock transition over δtj=tj+1−tj\delta t_j=t_{j+1}-t_j is

Stj+1=Stjexp{(μ−σ2/2)δtj+σδtjZj},Zj∼N(0,1).S_{t_{j+1}}=S_{t_j}\exp\{(\mu-\sigma^2/2)\delta t_j+\sigma\sqrt{\delta t_j}Z_j\},\quad Z_j\sim N(0,1).

Pricing uses ℚ\mathbb Q with drift rr. For τ=T−t>0\tau=T-t>0, set d1=[log(S/K)+(r+σ2/2)τ]/(στ)d_1=[\log(S/K)+(r+\sigma^2/2)\tau]/(\sigma\sqrt\tau) and d2=d1−στd_2=d_1-\sigma\sqrt\tau. Then

C=SΦ(d1)−Ke−rτΦ(d2),Δ=Φ(d1),Γ=ϕ(d1)Sστ.C=S\Phi(d_1)-Ke^{-r\tau}\Phi(d_2),\qquad \Delta=\Phi(d_1),\qquad \Gamma=\frac{\phi(d_1)}{S\sigma\sqrt\tau}.

Here Φ\Phi and ϕ\phi denote the standard normal cdf and density. Avoid computing delta or gamma at τ=0\tau=0. Terminal liquidation is not a new hedge decision.

2. Two equivalent cash ledgers

Let S̃j=e−rtjStj\widetilde S_j=e^{-rt_j}S_{t_j}, and let hjSh_j^{S} be the shares held from tjt_j to tj+1t_{j+1}. In a frictionless market, discounted terminal seller’s error equals

E=C(0,S0)+∑j=0n−1hjS(S̃j+1−S̃j)−e−rT(ST−K)+.E=C(0,S_0)+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}h_j^{S}(\widetilde S_{j+1}-\widetilde S_j)-e^{-rT}(S_T-K)^+.

This telescoping gains representation and the cash-account recursion in the proposal must agree path by path. Verify the identity using a separate implementation before interpreting simulations. Implement both representations separately.

The discounted traded notional, with liquidation, is

U=|h0S|S0+∑j=1n−1|hjS−hj−1S|S̃j+|hn−1S|S̃n.U=|h_0^{S}|S_0+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}|h_j^{S}-h_{j-1}^{S}|\widetilde S_j+|h_{n-1}^{S}|\widetilde S_n.

It is not a cost unless multiplied by a specified fee. Keep fee optimization out of this thesis’s baseline.

3. Local gamma error and convergence

Use the Black–Scholes PDE Ct+rSCS+12σ2S2CSS=rCC_t+rSC_S+\tfrac12\sigma^2S^2C_{SS}=rC and a second-order Taylor expansion. Over one hedge interval, the change in option value minus the change in the delta-and-cash portfolio is approximately 12Γj[(Sj+1−Sj)2−σ2Sj2δtj]\tfrac12\Gamma_j[(S_{j+1}-S_j)^2-\sigma^2 S_j^2 \delta t_j]. Thus the leading seller’s terminal error is

EΓ=−12∑j=0n−1e−rtj+1Γj[(Sj+1−Sj)2−σ2Sj2δtj].E_\Gamma=-\frac12\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}e^{-rt_{j+1}}\Gamma_j[(S_{j+1}-S_j)^2-\sigma^2S_j^2\delta t_j].

The approximation omits higher-order terms and is not an exact replication identity. High gamma near expiry can make finite-grid behavior depart from the asymptotic picture. Test the approximation against the exact ledger, not against another Taylor expansion.

For MM paths, report RMSÊ=M−1∑i=1MEi2\widehat{\mathrm{RMSE}}=\sqrt{M^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{M} E_i^2}. The convergence reference n−1/2n^{-1/2} is illustrative under regularity, not a fitted restriction. Replicate the entire experiment on independent path samples to assess uncertainty in slopes and tail statistics. Nested grids enable paired comparisons: frequencies must not use unrelated paths.
