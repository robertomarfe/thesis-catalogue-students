Covariance Shrinkage and Portfolio Performance

Does Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage improve out-of-sample global minimum-variance portfolio risk and turnover relative to sample covariance and equal weighting, and how does performance depend on estimation-window length?

Mathematical framework

For sample covariance SS, shrink toward scaled identity: Σ̂=(1−a)S+atr(S)I/N\widehat\Sigma=(1-a)S+a\operatorname{tr}(S)I/N.

Unconstrained GMV weights are proportional to Σ−1𝟏\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1; long-only requires constrained optimization. Positive uniform rescaling leaves weights invariant, not predicted variance. Document Ledoit-Wolf intensity and ML denominator versus sample ddof=1. Compare spectra, conditioning, risk and concentration. Record singular windows, not silent replacements. Use paired dates and drift-corrected costs.

Research checks

Document shrinkage target, intensity and denominator; compare eigenvalues, condition numbers, KKT, weights and predicted risk on common origins. Same costs and drift, equal weight and sample GMV; report singular windows and predefined fallback, compare paired net OOS variance/turnover and block intervals.
