Asset pricing · Fixed income and interest rates · Level 2/3

Data: Empirical core; actual rights, coverage and input validation pending. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Nominal and inflation-indexed yields jointly reveal market inflation compensation, but this is not a direct observation of expected inflation. Survey forecasts and uncertainty proxies help characterize its variation without assuming that the residual is a pure inflation risk premium.

Research question

How does the term structure of inflation compensation covary with survey inflation expectations and inflation uncertainty, and how much variation remains after accounting for available expectations proxies?

Economic mechanism

For compatible continuous zero yields at matched maturity, BEIt(n)=ytN(n)−ytR(n)BEI_t(n)=y_t^N(n)-y_t^R(n).

Research design

Match nominal and real fitted curves at 5 and 10 years on common dates and quarterly formation dates. Relate inflation compensation to documented inflation proxies, survey releases and trailing inflation volatility. Compute a survey residual only after establishing comparable horizons and units.

Verify nominal/real compounding, exact common dates, provider identity and release. Never claim a residual decomposition if the survey alignment fails. This thesis studies inflation compensation, not an ACM nominal premium.

Required exhibits

Nominal/real/BEI curve panels, identity checks, survey alignment and residual chart if feasible, conditional association table.

Minimum viable thesis

Matched nominal/real yields and BEI at two maturities, descriptive association with documented inflation proxy and uncertainty; no residual absent matched survey.

Scope and feasibility

Inflation compensation is not pure expected inflation; residual is not an identified inflation risk premium. Real fitted yields are not an OIS curve; TIPS liquidity and indexation matter. Survey expectations may be noisy and revised curve vintages are retrospective.

Prerequisites: Bond pricing, inflation measurement, time series, macro finance.

Optional extension: Compare an independently published inflation-risk-premium model if available with exact definitions, or study longer-horizon forward compensation.

Milestones

W1-3: theory/source definitions; W4-6: maturity and identity checks; W7-9: survey and regressions; W10-12: robustness; W13-16: writing.
