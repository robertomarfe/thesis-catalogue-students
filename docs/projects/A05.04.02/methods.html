Inflation Risk and the Term Structure of Nominal and Real Interest Rates

How does the term structure of inflation compensation covary with survey inflation expectations and inflation uncertainty, and how much variation remains after accounting for available expectations proxies?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Measurement

Breakeven inflation is a nominal-real zero yield difference; TIPS indexation, liquidity, deflation protection and fitted-curve conventions may matter. A stylized decomposition is BEI=Eπ+IRP+ℓ+convexityBEI=E\pi+IRP+\ell+\mathrm{convexity}, where signs and grouping depend on the convention. Only the sum is observed as a curve difference. CPI10 covers current and next nine calendar years, not automatically a rolling ten-year horizon. Short-horizon SPF forecasts cannot be subtracted from ten-year BEI as a matched expectation.

Association rather than identification

Compare nominal, real and compensation changes and their co-movement with the released proxy. Use trailing inflation volatility as realized historical uncertainty proxy, not expected conditional uncertainty. Without a documented horizon reconciliation, report co-movement only; even a matched residual is not a pure risk premium.

Research checks

Verify nominal/real compounding, exact common dates, provider identity and release. Never claim a residual decomposition if the survey alignment fails. This thesis studies inflation compensation, not an ACM nominal premium.
