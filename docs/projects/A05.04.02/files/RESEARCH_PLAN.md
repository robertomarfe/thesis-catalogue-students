# Research plan | A05.04.02

## Study design and independent work

Verify nominal/real compounding, exact common dates, provider identity and release. Never claim a residual decomposition if the survey alignment fails. This thesis studies inflation compensation, not an ACM nominal premium.

## Required tasks

1. Verify nominal/real and provider BEI identities with same maturity and compounding.

2. Construct term structure levels/slopes and compare nominal/real contributions.

3. Align survey expectations exactly, convert inflation units consistently, construct residual proxy if justified.

4. Relate BEI and residual to lagged realized inflation volatility and financial conditions, HAC/block intervals.

5. Compare pre/post-2008 and crisis windows; distinguish yield data revisions from observed transactions and liquidity explanations.

## Required exhibits

Nominal/real/BEI curve panels, identity checks, survey alignment and residual chart if feasible, conditional association table.

## Robustness

Inflation compensation is not pure expected inflation; residual is not an identified inflation risk premium. Real fitted yields are not an OIS curve; TIPS liquidity and indexation matter. Survey expectations may be noisy and revised curve vintages are retrospective.

## Minimum viable thesis

Matched nominal/real yields and BEI at two maturities, descriptive association with documented inflation proxy and uncertainty; no residual absent matched survey.

## Optional extension

Compare an independently published inflation-risk-premium model if available with exact definitions, or study longer-horizon forward compensation.

## Prerequisites

Bond pricing, inflation measurement, time series, macro finance.

## Thesis outline

1. Nominal and real pricing;

2. Inflation compensation;

3. Curve and survey alignment;

4. Macro associations;

5. Risk and liquidity caveats.

## Milestones

W1-3: theory/source definitions; W4-6: maturity and identity checks; W7-9: survey and regressions; W10-12: robustness; W13-16: writing.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
