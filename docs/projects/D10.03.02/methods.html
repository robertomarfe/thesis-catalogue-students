D10.03.02 · Derivatives · International, FX & commodities

Currency Hedging of US Equity Investments for Euro-Area Investors

Research question

When does a quarterly synthetic currency hedge improve the risk-return profile of a euro investor holding US equities? How do hedge ratio, equity-FX covariance and financing carry affect the result?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Currency and principal accounting

For initial EUR wealth one, buy S0S_0 USD of equities. Terminal EUR wealth without a hedge is (1+RUS)S0/S1(1+R_{US})S_0/S_1. Sell hS0hS_0 USD forward for EUR at FF USD/EUR: the incremental EUR payoff is hGhG with G=S0(1/F−1/S1)G=S_0(1/F-1/S_1). Thus the net pre-cost return is U+hGU+hG. For h=1h=1, the initial USD principal is hedged but future equity gains remain FX-sensitive. Use synthetic simple-CIP F=S0(1+rUSDτ)/(1+rEURτ)F=S_0(1+r_{USD}\tau)/(1+r_{EUR}\tau) with positive accrual factors.

Past-only hedge choice

If Var(G)>0\operatorname{Var}(G)>0, the unconstrained variance-minimizing hedge ratio is −Cov(U,G)/Var(G)-\operatorname{Cov}(U,G)/\operatorname{Var}(G). The prescribed benchmark clips to [0,1][0,1]; estimate with at least 40 completed quarters and test after the training cutoff. Compound total US market returns including RF before conversion; do not treat overlapping monthly three-month contracts as independent quarters. Record initial capital, forward notional, cost and basis assumptions, paired uncertainty and realized risk. These are synthetic proxy contracts, not executable hedge returns.

Research checks

Audit USD/EUR sign, full-principal identity, common dates, lagged rates and no look-ahead. Retain 60 aligned-quarter target; if unavailable agree a descriptive fixed-ratio scope. No ex-post optimized benchmark.
