Goal

Dependence affects a payoff on the average differently from a payoff on the minimum. The former depends on basket dispersion, while the latter is exposed to joint tail events. The project keeps marginal asset laws fixed and studies how admissible correlation changes alter both claims.

Research question: How do correlation and volatility heterogeneity affect arithmetic basket calls and worst-of puts, and when does the sign of correlation sensitivity differ?

Student-owned research

Keep discount and marginal inputs fixed; test PSD before every bump. Examine different strikes and volatilities rather than extrapolating one correlation sign. Distinguish tail dependence from linear correlation.

  1. Validate correlation matrix and single-asset/perfect-correlation limits.

  2. Price both contracts and geometric basket benchmark with MC intervals.

  3. Map price versus correlation, maturity and volatility heterogeneity.

  4. Estimate correlation sensitivity by paired finite differences with PSD checks and compare tail event probabilities.

  5. Stress dependence using a common-shock lognormal mixture with matched marginal distributions, clearly separate correlation from higher-order dependence.

Minimum viable thesis: Two assets, exact MC, both payoffs, five admissible correlations, perfect-correlation check and paired error bars.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
