Autocallable Notes: Pricing and Embedded Risk

How do autocall and downside barriers change fair coupon, expected life and tail loss, and how sensitive are valuations to volatility, dividends and issuer credit?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Contract and cashflow ledger

Set notional to one and Rj=Stj/SrefR_j=S_{t_j}/S_{\mathrm{ref}} with fixed issue reference. Redeem at the first eligible date with Rj≥1R_j\ge1, paying 1+jc1+jc. If never called, at maturity pay 1+mc1+mc when Rm≥BR_m\ge B, otherwise RmR_m with no coupon. Include threshold equality and a single mutually exclusive redemption cashflow. Define A,BcA,B_c as discounted principal and coupon loading. Under ℚ\mathbb Q value is 𝔼A+c𝔼Bc\mathbb E A+c\mathbb E B_c; a positive loading is necessary to solve for a coupon. If base PV exceeds par, a negative root is not a feasible nonnegative issue coupon.

Risk and uncertainty

Use exact observation-date sampling and paired bumps at fixed reference and contract terms. Discontinuities invalidate naive pathwise Greeks. Estimate coupon uncertainty as a ratio using paired or replicate observations. Distinguish Q contractual life from P loss and default termination. Independent zero-recovery default is a stylized optional scenario.

Research checks

Validate first-call, no-call, equality and maturity paths by hand. Freeze coupon before physical stress. Report ratio uncertainty and infeasibility; do not confuse a stylized price with an issue quote.
