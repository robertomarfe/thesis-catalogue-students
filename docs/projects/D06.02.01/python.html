D06.02.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

An American exercise right depends on the opportunity cost of waiting, forgone dividends and future payoff uncertainty. The focus is economic comparative statics, not algorithm rankings. The project studies the value of exercise for calls and puts and the response of the boundary to carry.

Research question: When is early exercise optimal and how much does the exercise right contribute to the value of American calls and puts?

Student-owned research

Use adjacent lattice refinements, verify q=0 with nonnegative r and the maturity limit. Report boundary resolution separately from price precision. Discrete dividends require different cashflow treatment.

  1. Derive backward induction and verify European prices against BSM.

  2. Compute American-minus-European premiums for calls and puts; map absolute and relative premiums by strike, carry and maturity (avoid ratios to near-zero values).

  3. Extract exercise boundaries at common dates and record indeterminate nodes close to indifference.

  4. Decompose the premium into discounted expected exercise benefits using the recursion V=continuation+max(intrinsic-continuation,0), and verify its sum under Q.

  5. Stress the rate/dividend trade-off and distinguish high intrinsic value from optimal exercise.

Minimum viable thesis: Validated tree, calls and puts, at least three carry scenarios, premium and boundary maps plus convergence. The focus is economic comparative statics rather than comparing solvers (D05.02.01).

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
