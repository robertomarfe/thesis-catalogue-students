Option Pricing under Model Misspecification: Implications for Prices and Hedging

How does fitting Black-Scholes to an at-the-money jump-diffusion price affect held-out option pricing and delta hedging across strikes and jump intensities?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Two measures and a common contract

Under the pricing measure ℚ\mathbb Q, let jump multiplier J=eYJ=e^Y with Y∼N(m,sJ2)Y\sim N(m,s_J^2), intensity λQ\lambda_Q, and aQ=𝔼ℚ[J]a_Q=\mathbb E^\mathbb Q[J]. With independent Brownian and Poisson shocks, dStSt−=(r−q−λQ(aQ−1))dt+σdWtQ+(J−1)dNtQ.\frac{dS_t}{S_{t-}}=(r-q-\lambda_Q(a_Q-1))dt+\sigma dW_t^Q+(J-1)dN_t^Q. Conditional on nn jumps over τ\tau, log variance is σ2τ+nsJ2\sigma^2\tau+ns_J^2 and mean stock is Fexp[−λQτ(aQ−1)]aQnF\exp[-\lambda_Q\tau(a_Q-1)]a_Q^n. Derive the price by weighting conditional lognormal call expectations; the stock-weighted Poisson tail matters as well as the count tail. Freeze the ATM convention (spot or forward) and the volatility fitted at inception for the benchmark hedge. A model delta holds its parameters fixed.

Wealth and hedge loss

Under ℙ\mathbb P, specify expected total return and jump intensity separately; the pricing intensity is not an estimate of physical frequency. Here q=0q=0 in the baseline. If a replicating portfolio of value VjV_j holds hjh_j shares just after rebalancing, cash is Bj=Vj−hjSjB_j=V_j-h_jS_j. Its next value is Vj+1=BjerΔt+hjSj+1.V_{j+1}=B_je^{r\Delta t}+h_jS_{j+1}. Both strategies start at the same true ℚ\mathbb Q option premium. Define discounted loss of the short option as e−rT[(ST−K)+−VT]e^{-rT}[(S_T-K)^+-V_T]. Report loss conditional on a jump and on no jump as well as unconditional loss. Model delta is not necessarily the minimum-variance hedge in an incomplete market. Do not refit implied volatility using held-out strikes or future paths.

Research checks

Freeze the ATM definition and calibration date. Record held-out prices separately from matched-path hedge losses. Build the cash ledger before changing physical jump intensity; do not equate a smaller time step with complete jump-risk replication.
