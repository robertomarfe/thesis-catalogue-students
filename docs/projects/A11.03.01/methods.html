Testing Euler Equations with Generalized Method of Moments

Research question

Moments and derivatives

Let θ=(β,γ)\theta=(\beta,\gamma) and m=βgc−γm=\beta g_c^{-\gamma}. For a payoff YY with price pp (gross bill p=1p=1, excess payoff p=0p=0), define u=zt(mY−p)u=z_t(mY-p). Derivatives of the scalar kernel are

∂βm=gc−γ,∂γm=−mloggc.\partial_\beta m=g_c^{-\gamma},\qquad \partial_\gamma m=-m\log g_c.
The sample Jacobian is the average of ztY∂θmz_tY\partial_\theta m. Retain the gross bill equation to identify the kernel scale; excess moments alone are homogeneous in β\beta. The ex post real bill is not conditionally riskless in real units.

GMM and identification

Minimize u‾(θ)⊤Wu‾(θ)\bar u(\theta)^\top W\bar u(\theta) using documented scales. Estimate long-run covariance with Bartlett HAC for the second step. Under correct specification, interior full-rank identification and efficient weighting, Tu‾⊤Wu‾T\bar u^\top W\bar u has asymptotic χq−p2\chi^2_{q-p} law, with qq moment count and pp parameter count. Weak rank, active bounds or regularization can invalidate this reference. Report profiles and failed starts.

Research checks

Use quarterly parameters and same-quarter consumption/payoffs; preserve the original broad bounds and fixed moments. Report scaling, HAC bandwidth, effective rank and convergence. Validate independently on a designed simulation before empirical estimation; do not select moments after viewing rejection.
