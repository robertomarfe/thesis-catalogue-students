Expected variance and implied variance

Under ℚ\mathbb Q use Heston variance dvt=κ(θ−vt)dt+ξvtdWtvdv_t=\kappa(\theta-v_t)dt+\xi\sqrt{v_t}dW_t^v and stock diffusion dSt/St=(r−q)dt+vtdWtSdS_t/S_t=(r-q)dt+\sqrt{v_t}dW_t^S, with correlation ρ\rho. The conditional mean solves a linear ODE, so 𝔼[vt]=θ+(v0−θ)e−κt\mathbb E[v_t]=\theta+(v_0-\theta)e^{-\kappa t} and

𝔼[∫0Tvtdt]=θT+(v0−θ)1−e−κTκ.\mathbb E\left[\int_0^T v_tdt\right]=\theta T+(v_0-\theta)\frac{1-e^{-\kappa T}}{\kappa}.
It is not generally ATM implied total variance. Specify all volatilities in annualized decimal units and distinguish the initial variance from its square root.

Smile features and conditioning

Let k=log(K/F)k=\log(K/F) and I(k,T)I(k,T) denote Black implied volatility. At a fixed maturity, estimate ATM level I(0,T)I(0,T), centered slope [I(h,T)−I(−h,T)]/(2h)[I(h,T)-I(-h,T)]/(2h) and curvature [I(h,T)−2I(0,T)+I(−h,T)]/h2[I(h,T)-2I(0,T)+I(-h,T)]/h^2. A smaller hh amplifies numerical inversion errors. Derive and validate the characteristic-function pricer or independent numerical reference. Form a finite-difference Jacobian of smile features with respect to appropriately scaled parameters; its singular values describe local conditioning, not global identification. For the joint ξ,ρ\xi,\rho experiment hold the expected variance curve fixed, and disclose any additional matching targets. Negative ρ\rho is a risk-neutral leverage mechanism, not direct physical evidence.

Research checks

First change one parameter at a time at a common forward and grid, then explore jointly varying correlation and vol-of-vol. Report actual expected variance for every scenario, flag price bounds, and assess slope sensitivity to the strike window.
