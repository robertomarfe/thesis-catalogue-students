A03.04.02 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Limited Market Participation and Consumption-Based Pricing

How does shareholder consumption exposure to aggregate dividend shocks affect the equity premium and risk-free rate in a transparent limited-participation pricing experiment?

Synthetic core · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Synthetic core. No external dataset required for the core design; optional extensions may require data.

OverviewResearch proposalTheory & methodsLiteratureData & sourcesPython labResearch plan

Research overview

Motivation

Asset pricing depends on the consumption exposure of the marginal investor. Changing shareholder consumption covariance with dividends can alter priced risk even when the aggregate dividend distribution stays fixed.

Minimum viable thesis

IID CRRA analytic benchmark and four shareholder scenarios with price existence and MC checks.

Prerequisites

Consumption-based pricing, lognormal moments, Python.

Methods

Equilibrium & preferences · Factor models & SDF · Cash-flow valuation · Simulation & Monte Carlo
