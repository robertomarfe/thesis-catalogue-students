## Motivation

Carry signals differ across bonds, commodities, currencies and other asset classes. A diversified carry composite can reveal whether these sleeves share risk-off exposure, but comparisons depend on the original strategy leverage and return conventions.

## Research question

How much common risk is present in published cross-asset carry returns, and does combining the asset-class sleeves reduce downside risk during stress?

## Economic mechanism

For documented carry sleeves $c_{j,t}$, a composite payoff is $c_{p,t}=\sum_j w_{j,t-1}c_{j,t}$.

## Scope and feasibility

Published strategy returns are not raw futures quotes or proof of carry predictability at instrument level. Do not assume all sleeves share leverage, costs or currency conventions. Revised/backfilled series and limited crisis counts constrain inference. Rights and workbook contents pending.
