Data: Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage. Status: current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

Motivation

Carry signals differ across bonds, commodities, currencies and other asset classes. A diversified carry composite can reveal whether these sleeves share risk-off exposure, but comparisons depend on the original strategy leverage and return conventions.

Research question

How much common risk is present in published cross-asset carry returns, and does combining the asset-class sleeves reduce downside risk during stress?

Economic mechanism

For documented carry sleeves cj,tc_{j,t}, a composite payoff is cp,t=∑jwj,t−1cj,tc_{p,t}=\sum_j w_{j,t-1}c_{j,t}.

Research design

Obtain the original KMPV strategy-return workbook under the conditional gate, document at least three distinct asset-class sleeves and form their native monthly intersection. Compare unscaled and past-volatility-scaled composites and paired stress outcomes. Keep any updated workbook separate.

Confirm original workbook, documented units, at least three distinct sleeves, no composites or silent splicing. If the core is unavailable, defer. Record stress proxy timing, notional and common dates.

Required exhibits

Workbook crosswalk; carry return and correlation table; PCA/loadings; composite wealth and stress losses; robustness by sleeve/sample.

Minimum viable thesis

Original workbook with at least three distinct asset-class sleeves, common-sample correlation and diversified composite, paired stress analysis; defer if file unavailable. Distinct from currency-only A09.02.02 and multi-asset trend-following A09.04.01.

Scope and feasibility

Published strategy returns are not raw futures quotes or proof of carry predictability at instrument level. Do not assume all sleeves share leverage, costs or currency conventions. Revised/backfilled series and limited crisis counts constrain inference. Rights and workbook contents pending.

Prerequisites: Multi-asset pricing, portfolio statistics, PCA and Python.

Optional extension: Compare with VME/TSMOM or AQR century carry data; analyze factor spanning and common downturns.

Milestones

W1-3: theory/file gate; W4-6: crosswalk; W7-9: correlation and composites; W10-12: stress and robustness; W13-16: writing and replication.
