Research plan | A09.03.02

Study design and independent work

Confirm original workbook, documented units, at least three distinct sleeves, no composites or silent splicing. If the core is unavailable, defer. Record stress proxy timing, notional and common dates.

Required tasks

  1. Inspect workbook labels, distinguish asset-class carry sleeves from global factor, confirm currency and scaling.

  2. Compare means, volatility, skewness, correlations and PCA on common dates, without averaging missing sleeves silently.

  3. Construct equal-weight and trailing inverse-volatility sleeve composites with fixed allocation rules; assess risk-adjusted returns and drawdowns.

  4. Evaluate joint loss frequencies and conditional correlations in NBER recessions or lagged stress regimes; block-bootstrap dates jointly.

  5. Test scaling, sample changes and exclusion of option sleeve; compare updated international/commodity histories without assuming identical methodology.

Required exhibits

Workbook crosswalk; carry return and correlation table; PCA/loadings; composite wealth and stress losses; robustness by sleeve/sample.

Robustness

Published strategy returns are not raw futures quotes or proof of carry predictability at instrument level. Do not assume all sleeves share leverage, costs or currency conventions. Revised/backfilled series and limited crisis counts constrain inference. Rights and workbook contents pending.

Minimum viable thesis

Original workbook with at least three distinct asset-class sleeves, common-sample correlation and diversified composite, paired stress analysis; defer if file unavailable. Distinct from currency-only A09.02.02 and multi-asset trend-following A09.04.01.

Optional extension

Compare with VME/TSMOM or AQR century carry data; analyze factor spanning and common downturns.

Prerequisites

Multi-asset pricing, portfolio statistics, PCA and Python.

Thesis outline

  1. Carry across markets;

  2. Published factor data;

  3. Diversification and common risk;

  4. Stress performance;

  5. Limits.

Milestones

W1-3: theory/file gate; W4-6: crosswalk; W7-9: correlation and composites; W10-12: stress and robustness; W13-16: writing and replication.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
