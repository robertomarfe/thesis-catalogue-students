Research plan | A01.02.02

Study design and independent work

Use the same base SMB within each nested pair and same RF for long-only test assets. Report two separate panels and paired uncertainty; distinguish momentum-formation sorts from evidence outside those sorts, without calling industries statistically independent.

Required tasks

  1. Describe factor correlations and momentum decile/size-sort gradients.

  2. Estimate FF3/FF5 spanning regression for Mom and compare residual means.

  3. Fit each nested model on identical dates, report annualized alphas, RMS alpha, GRS with assumptions and common-date block-bootstrap intervals for differences.

  4. Compare momentum-sorted and industry portfolios; quantify improvements outside the factor construction target.

  5. Check sensitivity to crisis months and subsamples, keeping SMB definitions consistent within each nested pair.

Required exhibits

Spanning and pricing error tables, alpha heatmaps, nested-model comparison, GRS/conditioning diagnostics and stability estimates.

Robustness

Momentum-sorted portfolios share construction ingredients with Mom, so fit can be mechanical; industry evidence is not fully independent at stock level. Higher R2 is not evidence of lower pricing errors. Do not assert FF3 strictly nested in FF5. No causal risk/behavioral identification.

Minimum viable thesis

Both nested pairs, Mom spanning, two test panels, common sample alpha comparisons and robust uncertainty. Distinct from A01.02.01 FF3 vs FF5 and A12.03.01 momentum/reversal payoffs.

Optional extension

Compare additional publicly available test assets or momentum crash states using predeclared dates; distinguish factor spanning from economic explanations.

Prerequisites

Asset pricing factors, multivariate regression, inference and Python.

Thesis outline

  1. Momentum and factor pricing;

  2. Spanning and pricing tests;

  3. Data and common samples;

  4. Nested comparisons;

  5. Robustness and interpretation.

Milestones

W1-3: literature and hypotheses; W4-6: data alignment; W7-9: spanning and pricing regressions; W10-12: tests and stability; W13-16: write and reproduce.
