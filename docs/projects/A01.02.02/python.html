Goal

Momentum is a useful test of whether fundamental factor models price return patterns not already spanned by their factors. Momentum-sorted portfolios and industries provide different test-asset constructions but share underlying securities. A successful addition need not identify a structural risk.

Research question: Does a traded momentum factor improve the pricing of momentum portfolios and independent industry portfolios relative to FF3 and FF5, and how stable is the improvement?

Setup check

Check an artificial factor spread is a difference, not a total return to subtract RF from. This artificial example does not validate the full research design or actual data.

long,short,rf=.025,.018,.002
spread=long-short
assert abs(spread-.007)<1e-12 and spread!=(long-short-rf)
print('Setup check passed.')

Student-owned research

Use the same base SMB within each nested pair and same RF for long-only test assets. Report two separate panels and paired uncertainty; distinguish momentum-formation sorts from evidence outside those sorts, without calling industries statistically independent.

  1. Describe factor correlations and momentum decile/size-sort gradients.

  2. Estimate FF3/FF5 spanning regression for Mom and compare residual means.

  3. Fit each nested model on identical dates, report annualized alphas, RMS alpha, GRS with assumptions and common-date block-bootstrap intervals for differences.

  4. Compare momentum-sorted and industry portfolios; quantify improvements outside the factor construction target.

  5. Check sensitivity to crisis months and subsamples, keeping SMB definitions consistent within each nested pair.

Minimum viable thesis: Both nested pairs, Mom spanning, two test panels, common sample alpha comparisons and robust uncertainty. Distinct from A01.02.01 FF3 vs FF5 and A12.03.01 momentum/reversal payoffs.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
