Forecasting Equity Market Volatility: GARCH versus Historical Volatility

Research question

Does GARCH(1,1) improve genuinely out-of-sample forecasts of daily and 21-trading-day market variance relative to rolling historical variance? Is the ranking stable in high- and low-volatility periods?

Student-owned research

  1. Plot returns and squared returns, estimate autocorrelations and ARCH diagnostics; fix forecast origins and loss evaluation dates. 2. Fit GARCH on training only (omega>0, alpha,beta>=0 and alpha+beta<1); record failures and compare with constant and rolling-variance benchmarks. 3. Generate recursive forecasts at t for t+1 and cumulative t+1 to t+21. Score QLIKE=log(h)+RV/h (positive forecast floor declared), MSE and forecast bias. 4. Compare losses via HAC inference or block bootstrap; evaluate 21-day nonoverlapping dates as a robustness check and display loss by regimes defined using lagged information.

Minimum viable thesis: One clean daily series; rolling historical and GARCH forecasts at both horizons on a common evaluation sample; QLIKE and MSE, uncertainty-aware loss comparisons, and honest treatment of noisy targets. No requirement that GARCH wins.
