A01.03.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage.

Goal

Many published anomalies share securities, signals and sample periods. A credible comparison of model-adjusted returns requires a fixed universe and an explicit treatment of multiplicity, not a ranking selected after inspecting t-statistics.

Research question: Which prespecified replicated anomalies retain nonzero alphas after common factor adjustment and dependence-aware multiplicity controls?

Student-owned research

Use one shared revision and record a separate SHA-256 for each OSAP file; the October 2026 route and metadata are inherited candidates, not verified. Freeze the coverage-only rule before outcomes; report exclusions, common calendar, sign and multiple-testing family. Resample entire date slices, preserving cross-anomaly dependence.

  1. Audit signal dictionary, units, missing codes and sign; freeze universe and time splits.

  2. Compute raw returns and alpha estimates under each benchmark on same months, HAC SE.

  3. Apply BH and conservative BY to two-sided alpha p-values within declared family; report discoveries without ranking by unadjusted t alone.

  4. Validate preselected survivors in 2010-2025; account for multiple tests there, report shrinkage/replication rates.

  5. Bootstrap dates jointly across anomalies, simulate correlated null to assess false discoveries; sensitivity to sample and factor overlap.

Minimum viable thesis: Pinned OSAP dictionary and returns, prespecified feasible universe, FF3/5+Mom alpha and multiplicity comparison, common held-out dates. Distinct from A12.01.01 specific liquidity signals.
