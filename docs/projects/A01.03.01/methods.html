Which Stock Return Anomalies Survive Standard Factor Adjustment?

Which prespecified replicated anomalies retain nonzero alphas after common factor adjustment and dependence-aware multiplicity controls?

Theory & methods

Open methods.pdfOpen methods.md

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Returns and the test family

Long-short payoffs LiL_i are zero-investment spreads; do not subtract the bill return again. Freeze signed signal definitions and eligibility using metadata and coverage only. Test factor-adjusted intercepts using dependence-aware standard errors for the time series. Declare the family of mm hypotheses before testing.

Multiple testing

Order p-values p(1)≤⋯≤p(m)p_{(1)}\le\cdots\le p_{(m)}. At FDR target qq, BH selects through the largest kk with p(k)≤kq/mp_{(k)}\le kq/m (zero discoveries if none). BY replaces qq by q/Hmq/H_m, where Hm=∑j=1m1/jH_m=\sum_{j=1}^m1/j. BH needs independence or appropriate positive dependence; BY accommodates arbitrary dependence of valid p-values but is conservative. FDR concerns expected false-discovery proportion, not probability of any false discovery. Distinguish discovery from a later frozen replication sample; neither is a claim of implementable net profits.
