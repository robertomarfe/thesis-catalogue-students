Utility Indifference Pricing of Nonreplicable Claims

How do the unhedgeable component and risk aversion determine the utility indifference price relative to complete-market and actuarial benchmarks?

Research overview

Motivation

Incomplete markets allow a claim to have different values to buyers and sellers who cannot trade away its residual risk. Exponential utility isolates the effect of risk aversion and the hedgeable component in a one-period economy, providing an economically interpretable comparison with actuarial and replication benchmarks.

Minimum viable thesis

Gaussian derivation and independent optimizer, replicability limit, risk-aversion and correlation sensitivity, one nonlinear finite-state claim if time permits.

Prerequisites

Expected utility, asset pricing, Gaussian moments, convex optimization.

Methods

Constrained optimization · Equilibrium & preferences · Hedging & trading costs · Tail risk & distributions
