Option Pricing under Stochastic Volatility: The Heston Model

When does stochastic volatility improve pricing beyond constant volatility, and which dimensions of a cross-section of European options identify its parameters? Assess the hypothesis that strike and maturity coverage jointly improve held-out pricing, even when parameter recovery remains weak.

Heston dynamics and a pricing reference

Under ℚ\mathbb Q, let dSt/St=(r−q)dt+vtdWtS,dvt=κ(θ−vt)dt+ξvtdWtv,dS_t/S_t=(r-q)dt+\sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t^S,\qquad dv_t=\kappa(\theta-v_t)dt+\xi\sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t^v, with instantaneous correlation ρ\rho, initial variance v0>0v_0>0, mean reversion κ>0\kappa>0, long-run variance θ>0\theta>0, and vol-of-vol ξ>0\xi>0. The condition 2κθ≥ξ22\kappa\theta\ge\xi^2 ensures an inaccessible zero boundary from positive variance in the standard CIR process; its violation is not by itself a failed calibration.

With XT=log(ST/F)X_T=\log(S_T/F), the characteristic function φ(u)=𝔼Q[eiuXT]\varphi(u)=\mathbb E^Q[e^{iuX_T}] has form exp(A+Bv0)\exp(A+Bv_0). The Riccati system (time to maturity) is B′=12ξ2B2+(iρξu−κ)B−12(u2+iu),A′=κθB,B'=\tfrac12\xi^2B^2+(i\rho\xi u-\kappa)B-\tfrac12(u^2+iu),\quad A'=\kappa\theta B, with zero initial conditions. Derive a Fourier representation and build an independent pricing check, including the constant-variance limit when v0=θv_0=\theta and ξ→0\xi\to0.

Identification is not prediction

Let 𝒯\mathcal T be training quotes and si>0s_i>0 fixed scales determined from training information (e.g., vega with a floor). Minimize ∑i∈𝒯[(Ci(ϑ)−Ciobs)/si]2\sum_{i\in\mathcal T}[(C_i(\vartheta)-C_i^{obs})/s_i]^2 for the parameter vector ϑ\vartheta. Freeze the split and scales, record objective profiles and singular values of a scaled residual Jacobian. Similar objective values may correspond to very different parameters. Independent holdouts in strikes and maturity evaluate pricing, not identification; a flat-volatility truth is a meaningful negative control.

Research checks

Begin with a checked pricer and a two-parameter fit, then release all five parameters. Freeze an entire maturity and alternating strikes before generating noise. Report objective profiles, failed starts, parameter recovery and held-out prices as separate exhibits.
