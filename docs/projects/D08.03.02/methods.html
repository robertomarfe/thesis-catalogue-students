Put Writing and the Compensation for Downside Risk

Funding

With zero dividends, parity equates S0−C0S_0-C_0 and Ke−rT−P0Ke^{-rT}-P_0. Cash-secured writing requires collateral and receives premium; both determine initial funding. Rolling index returns do not isolate variance premium.

Tail comparison

A lagged equity weight gives return Rtf+wt−1(RtM−Rtf)R^f_t+w_{t-1}(R^M_t-R^f_t). Disclose weight bounds and realized mismatch. Compute tail loss on common months with a consistent loss sign. Turbulence labels based on future returns are descriptive; lagged VIX regimes are a different, feasible design. Separate launch and backfill.
