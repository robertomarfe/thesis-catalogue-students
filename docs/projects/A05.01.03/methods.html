Interest Rate Volatility and the Performance of Affine Term Structure Models

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Conditional variance

Vasicek yields Vh=σ2(1−e−2κh)/(2κ)V_h=\sigma^2(1-e^{-2\kappa h})/(2\kappa). CIR adds a term linear in the current rate, as in the proposal. Estimate conditional means consistently and define a variance outcome from squared forecast innovations, not squared levels. Score positive variance forecasts using a common loss such as logV+e2/V\log V+e^2/V. Respect irregular calendar gaps.

From rate to yield risk

If yj=aj+bjry_j=a_j+b_j r, conditional innovation covariance is bb⊤Vhbb^\top V_h for b=(b1,…,bm)⊤b=(b_1,\ldots,b_m)^\top. This is a one-factor restriction. Physical rate estimates do not identify the risk-neutral loadings; either use separately documented illustrative loadings or a training-only empirical projection. Interpret unexplained covariance rather than forcing all observed yield shocks into the short-rate factor.
