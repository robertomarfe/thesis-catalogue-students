Reading focus: Reference-dependent sell pressure in a reduced-form clearing market with common fundamental news.

Fixing mean propensity across investors does not match realized turnover, inventory distribution or cash budgets. The dealer may short and investors have no explicit cash constraint; no prospect-theory equilibrium or empirical disposition-effect identification. Return predictability should use lagged overhang, not contemporaneous price-defined overhang.
