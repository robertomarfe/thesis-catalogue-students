A12.03.02 · Asset Pricing · Portfolios & market frictions

The Disposition Effect and Return Predictability: A Simulation Study

Can asymmetric selling relative to investors' purchase reference prices produce short-run underreaction or subsequent return reversals in a stylized market with identical fundamental news?

Synthetic core · Level 3/3 · Research draft

Synthetic core. No external dataset required for the core design; optional extensions may require data.

OverviewResearch proposalTheory & methodsLiteratureData & sourcesPython labResearch plan

Research overview

Motivation

Reference-dependent selling may generate asymmetric supply following news. A stylized clearing market isolates the mechanism without claiming a preference equilibrium.

Minimum viable thesis

Audited market clearing and share accounting, symmetric/asymmetric/no-reference controls, replicated paths, news response and predictability metrics with uncertainty.

Prerequisites

Behavioral finance, probability, numerical root finding, simulation and basic regression.

Methods

Stochastic processes · Simulation & Monte Carlo · Regression & inference
