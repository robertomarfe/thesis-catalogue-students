**Asset pricing · Portfolio choice, behavioral finance and market frictions · Level 3/3**

**Data:** Synthetic core; no mandatory external data. **Status:** current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

## Motivation

Reference-dependent selling may generate asymmetric supply following news. A stylized clearing market isolates the mechanism without claiming a preference equilibrium.

## Research question

Can asymmetric selling relative to investors' purchase reference prices produce short-run underreaction or subsequent return reversals in a stylized market with identical fundamental news?

## Economic mechanism

Investor order $q_i(P)=\max(a_i(F-P)/P_- -s_i h_i,-h_i)$, with previous price $P_-$ and holdings $h_i$.

## Research design

N=100, 200 matched paths, 500 dates/burn 100; no-reference, symmetric/asymmetric controls, independent turnover calibration; positive fundamentals, price admission, news response and lagged prediction.

Check monotone market clearing, nonnegative holdings and exact inventory accounting; reproduce purchase-weighted reference update and no-reference/symmetric/asymmetric controls with identical shocks. Add explicit matched-turnover calibration on development paths only, report unmatched paths and paired simulation intervals; use strictly lagged predictive variables. No claim on actual investor data.

## Required exhibits

Documented simulation rules, accounting and clearing tests, price-gap trajectories, return autocorrelation and predictability distributions, turnover and sensitivity plots.

## Minimum viable thesis

Audited market clearing and share accounting, symmetric/asymmetric/no-reference controls, replicated paths, news response and predictability metrics with uncertainty.

## Scope and feasibility

No empirical causal inference or claim that stylized demand is structurally estimated. Avoid interpreting price pressure as risk premium without an SDF. Keep fundamental shocks identical across treatments and define reference updates for new/zero holdings. Report sensitivity to market-maker and positivity rules.

**Prerequisites:** Behavioral finance, probability, numerical root finding, simulation and basic regression.

**Optional extension:** Add heterogeneous beliefs or endogenous participation as a separate mechanism; compare price pressure and return predictability holding disposition parameters fixed.
