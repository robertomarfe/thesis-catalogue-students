Different option markets

VIX and VVIX are annualized index-point measures from different option markets. Neither is a realized return or an observation of physical vol-of-vol. The broad equity proxy differs from SPX. Do not subtract the indices to estimate a premium.

Timing and score

At close tt, pt=(1+e−xt⊤b)−1p_t=(1+e^{-x_t^\top b})^{-1} uses only information available then. The outcome is known after five subsequent audited sessions. Standardize within training; choose models on validation. Compare Brier loss (Dt−pt)2(D_t-p_t)^2, calibration and event counts on common test dates. Purge labels crossing splits.
