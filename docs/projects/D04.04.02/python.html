Goal

Transparent regression hedges offer a structural-model alternative when model parameters are unavailable. Their value depends on observable features that predict useful conditional covariances. The question is whether a richer feasible basis improves replication on independent paths.

Research question: Can transparent regression hedges improve on BSM delta when the underlying follows a stochastic-volatility or jump process and the policy is trained without access to true model parameters?

Student-owned research

Distinguish quadratic regression hedges from neural entropic-risk optimization. Test future-input perturbation, rank and conditioning, validation-only penalty choice, paired test squared loss and tails.

  1. Verify cash ledger and frictionless GBM convergence.

  2. Fit basis regression without look-ahead; select ridge on validation.

  3. Freeze and evaluate on independent paths, compare RMSE, ES95 and turnover.

  4. Test added lagged volatility features and independent stress DGP with common premium within each comparison.

  5. Report sensitivity to training size, basis degree and seed.

Minimum viable thesis: GBM and one misspecified DGP, linear and enriched basis, independent test and BSM benchmark.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
