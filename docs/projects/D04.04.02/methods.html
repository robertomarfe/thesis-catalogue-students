D04.04.02 · Derivatives · Hedging & volatility derivatives

Hedging with Model-Free Regression Policies

Can transparent regression hedges improve on BSM delta when the underlying follows a stochastic-volatility or jump process and the policy is trained without access to true model parameters?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Linear features and identification

Let hi=ai⊤fih_i=a_i^\top f_i, where fif_i is observable at trading time. The regression design uses products fiΔS̃if_i\Delta\widetilde S_i, but the future gain is not a policy input. Use training-only scaling and explicitly handle constant columns. At time zero the state is deterministic; redundant feature coefficients may not be uniquely interpretable. Check realized positions and gains, not just coefficients.

Economic benchmark

For adapted square-integrable policies, discounted martingale gains have mean zero. Do not extend this statement to a drifted physical market. Use a common clean premium per scenario. True latent model parameters may enter an oracle comparison only, not a feasible policy.

Research checks

Distinguish quadratic regression hedges from neural entropic-risk optimization. Test future-input perturbation, rank and conditioning, validation-only penalty choice, paired test squared loss and tails.
