Conditional variance window

Under constant Q CIR parameters and a continuous stock diffusion, conditional expected annualized variance from TT to T+ΔT+\Delta is A+BvTA+Bv_T. With deterministic rates, the model futures price is 100𝔼0ℚA+BvT100\mathbb E_0^{\mathbb Q}\sqrt{A+Bv_T}. Jensen bounds it above by 100A+B𝔼0ℚvT100\sqrt{A+B\mathbb E_0^{\mathbb Q}v_T}.

Term structure

At delivery zero the price equals model spot VIX. Long delivery converges to the stationary-law expectation for positive mean reversion and long-run variance. Feller violation allows visits to zero, not failure of the exact transition. Hold κ,θ\kappa,\theta fixed when isolating ξ\xi. The continuous diffusion construction is a model approximation to the index methodology.
