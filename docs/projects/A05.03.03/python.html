Current review draft; approval pending. Empirical core; actual rights, coverage and input validation pending.

Goal

Forward rates embed both expectations and risk compensation. Survey forecasts may contribute independently measured information about future growth and inflation. The study evaluates incremental pseudo-out-of-sample predictability conditional on a yield-only benchmark and documented survey releases.

Research question: Do real GDP growth and inflation expectations from the Survey of Professional Forecasters improve pseudo-out-of-sample forecasts of future Treasury excess returns beyond forward-rate factors?

Student-owned research

Require release and horizon metadata before estimation. If necessary use the original minimum of one released horizon and two maturities; do not substitute dispersion for expected growth. Label the result pseudo-OOS unless vintages are truly reconstructible.

  1. Audit forecasts, release dates, growth conventions and GSW return construction.

  2. Construct yield-only features and SPF features, same sample; verify label timing.

  3. Estimate nested predictive models with train-only standardization and mature outcomes.

  4. Compare held-out MSPE, OOS R2 against common historical mean, paired errors and overlapping-horizon HAC/block inference.

  5. Study incremental growth vs inflation, 3/12-month horizons, crisis exclusion and vintage limitations.

Minimum viable thesis: One released SPF horizon, two maturities, historical mean/yield-only/augmented forecasts, overlap-aware held-out scoring.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
