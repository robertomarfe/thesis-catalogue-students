Goal

A scheme that prices a smooth claim accurately may oscillate near a vanilla kink or a digital discontinuity. Even a small price error can conceal large Greek errors. The project isolates stability, monotonicity and convergence of time-stepping schemes in the same European pricing problem.

Research question: How do explicit, backward-Euler and Crank-Nicolson methods differ in stability, oscillations and accuracy when the option payoff is nonsmooth?

Student-owned research

Compare analytical prices and Greeks over spot, not just ATM. A-stability does not imply positivity or accuracy. Exclude deliberately unstable runs from successful time-to-tolerance comparisons.

  1. Derive stencil, stability/positivity conditions and boundary treatment.

  2. Implement each scheme; unit-test zero payoff, constant payoff discount and BSM prices.

  3. Sweep dt/dx^2 to demonstrate instability deliberately, not use unstable prices as candidates.

  4. Compare price, delta, gamma max/RMS errors near strike and across interior grid; plot oscillations for short maturities and Rannacher smoothing.

  5. Separate time, space and truncation errors with one-at-a-time refinement and runtime/error frontiers.

Minimum viable thesis: Three schemes plus Rannacher variant, European call and digital, analytical references, stable/unstable grids, separate refinement and Greek diagnostics. Distinct from D05.02.01’s American optimal-stopping solver comparison.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
