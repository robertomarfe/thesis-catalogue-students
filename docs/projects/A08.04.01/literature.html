Reading focus: Scenario ES constraint versus variance and equal weight under matched training risk budgets.

Fractional empirical tail mass matters for small samples; ES is not VaR or a guaranteed loss ceiling. A Gaussian or t sample of simple returns may contain inadmissible gross returns. Dense LP uses quadratic memory in scenario count; optimizer failures and infeasibility differ. Equal risk-budget comparison is not numerical equality of ES and variance.
