Width versus resolution

Cumulants motivate truncation intervals but do not guarantee error bounds. Vary width separately from NN against independent BSM and converged Poisson-mixture prices. Retain negative computed prices in the diagnostics. Include coefficient setup in fair timing.

Research checks

Inspect short maturity, rare jumps and deep strikes before asserting convergence. A wider interval may require more terms; do not claim universal exponential convergence.
