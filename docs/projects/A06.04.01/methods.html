A06.04.01 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Equity and Bond Yield Slopes over the Business Cycle

Research question

How do matched-maturity equity and nominal Treasury yield slopes covary across the US business cycle, and is the relation stable across alternative measures of economic conditions?

Conditional data access · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Conditional data access. GKK archive and matching bond/macro time series.

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

Use matched maturities stE=et(10)−et(2)s^E_t=e_t(10)-e_t(2) and stB=bt(10)−bt(2)s^B_t=b_t(10)-b_t(2).

With retrospective recession indicator ItI_t, estimate stE=a+bstB+cIt+dstBIt+uts^E_t=a+b s^B_t+c I_t+d s^B_t I_t+u_t. Expansion association is bb, recession association b+db+d; report the latter with its joint covariance. Distinguish regression on levels from changes; preserve persistence in joint calendar blocks and count recession episodes. Use alternative maturity pairs and lagged industrial production growth as prespecified; retrospective labels are not signals available to investors. Nominal-real mismatch cannot isolate inflation risk.

Research checks

Verify exact GKK/GSW grid 2/10y with dates and units, distinguish level regression from change regression, compare interaction coefficient with prespecified alternative maturity pairs and lagged IP growth. Resample joint date blocks, report recession episode counts and leave-one-episode sensitivity; no causal identification or real-time prediction.
