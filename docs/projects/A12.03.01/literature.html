Reading focus: Published momentum and reversal spreads differ in formation signal and orientation, not a stock-level strategy replication.

Momentum winner-minus-loser and reversal loser-minus-winner require explicit orientation; formation lookback is not holding horizon. Provider factor vintage and reinvestment conventions may differ; a common return sample does not establish same stock universe or turnover. Avoid interpreting difference in separately significant alphas as significant difference.
