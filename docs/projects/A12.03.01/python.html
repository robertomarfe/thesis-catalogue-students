Goal

Momentum and reversal reflect different formation horizons; published spreads differ in orientation, not necessarily holding horizon.

Research question: Do published portfolios sorted on past returns show systematically different average spreads and factor-adjusted returns across formation windows, and how stable are the differences across market regimes?

Setup check

Check orientation and difference. This artificial example does not validate the full research design or actual data.

import math
mom,rev=.02,.01
assert math.isclose(mom-rev,.01) and math.isclose(mom+rev,.03)
print('Setup check passed.')

Student-owned research

Verify ST, Mom and LT definitions and exact units; align common dates and test the paired return/alpha difference directly with HAC/blocks. Freeze pre/post-1990 dates and report dependent returns; do not claim individual-level trading evidence or use future selection.

  1. Verify orientation, formation and holding windows; reproduce spread means and autocorrelations on common months.

  2. Estimate CAPM and FF3 alphas, with HAC intervals for each and paired difference tests between continuation spreads.

  3. Analyze optional decile portfolio mean/alpha profiles across formation windows, compare value versus equal-weight if files support and distinguish factor vs decile construction.

  4. Prespecify subperiod and recession sensitivity using available NBER dates (optional), report covariance and severe loss episodes; distinguish turnover-sensitive short reversal from long reversal.

Minimum viable thesis: Verified three factor files and FF3, aligned formation orientation, CAPM/FF3 and paired comparisons, prespecified subperiod stability, careful distinction of formation versus holding horizon. Decile analysis desirable but not mandatory.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
