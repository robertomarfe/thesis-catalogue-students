Momentum and Long-Term Reversal: Competing Return Patterns

Research question

Do published portfolios sorted on past returns show systematically different average spreads and factor-adjusted returns across formation windows, and how stable are the differences across market regimes?

Mathematical framework

For common-date difference dt=rtA−rtBd_t=r^A_t-r^B_t, identical factor regressors give α̂d=α̂A−α̂B\widehat\alpha_d=\widehat\alpha_A-\widehat\alpha_B.

ST and LT reversal are low-minus-high, momentum high-minus-low. Preserve published orientation and label any reversed continuation comparison. All hold one month. Estimate paired differences directly, not subtracting independent standard errors. Align vintage and common sample; freeze subperiods, do not infer stock-level turnover.

Research checks

Verify ST, Mom and LT definitions and exact units; align common dates and test the paired return/alpha difference directly with HAC/blocks. Freeze pre/post-1990 dates and report dependent returns; do not claim individual-level trading evidence or use future selection.
