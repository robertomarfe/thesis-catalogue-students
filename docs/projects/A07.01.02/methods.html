Variance Risk Premia and Economic Uncertainty

Is a consistently measured 30-day variance-premium proxy associated with economic policy uncertainty beyond the level of expected physical variance and financial stress?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Separate the two variance legs

Let RVt,30RV_{t,30} be the sum of squared log returns during the next 30 calendar days. The annualized physical forecast is (365/30)Etℙ[RVt,30](365/30)E_t^{\mathbb P}[RV_{t,30}]; the risk-neutral leg is approximated by squared decimal VIX. Forecast using only matured labels and predeclared historical/HAR-style regressors. Use the same units for both legs and show sensitivity to physical forecast error. VIX and the broad FF market proxy have different underlying definitions.

Association and uncertainty

Estimate a relation of the form VRPt=a+blogEPUt−ℓ+cPt+dNFCIt−ℓ′+etVRP_t=a+b\log EPU_{t-\ell}+cP_t+dNFCI_{t-\ell'}+e_t. Use lags reflecting availability, not a convenient observation-date join; revised data cannot establish actual real-time performance. Re-estimate the generated physical forecast inside joint time blocks where feasible. The regression is descriptive/conditional, not an instrumented causal design.

Research checks

Verify rights for EPU and other series. Keep a release-date ledger, include a predeclared crisis sensitivity, and report forecast uncertainty alongside association. A positive or negative association is not itself a causal finding or a return premium.
