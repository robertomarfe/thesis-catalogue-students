Goal

Risk-neutral variance may rise because expected physical risk increases, because investors require more compensation for bearing risk, or both. This project asks whether a measured variance-premium proxy is associated with policy uncertainty after separating these channels statistically.

Research question: Is a consistently measured 30-day variance-premium proxy associated with economic policy uncertainty beyond the level of expected physical variance and financial stress?

Student-owned research

Verify rights for EPU and other series. Keep a release-date ledger, include a predeclared crisis sensitivity, and report forecast uncertainty alongside association. A positive or negative association is not itself a causal finding or a return premium.

  1. Match VIX, market and 30-day future labels; document trading-day and annualization conventions.

  2. Compare rolling variance and HAR-style forecasts with QLIKE and MSE on held-out dates.

  3. Form VRP with prequential physical forecasts, estimate relation to lagged log EPU controlling for expected variance and lagged NFCI.

  4. Contrast with ex-post VIX-squared minus realized variance and quantify forecast uncertainty via time blocks/refitting.

  5. Examine crisis influence, alternate horizon proxies and common support; use HAC and paired calendar blocks.

Minimum viable thesis: One verified 30-day proxy, two physical forecasts and conditional association to EPU on held-out dates, with clear units and uncertainty.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
