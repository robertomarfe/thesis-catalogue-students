Motivation

Risk-neutral variance may rise because expected physical risk increases, because investors require more compensation for bearing risk, or both. This project asks whether a measured variance-premium proxy is associated with policy uncertainty after separating these channels statistically.

Research question

Is a consistently measured 30-day variance-premium proxy associated with economic policy uncertainty beyond the level of expected physical variance and financial stress?

Economic mechanism

In annualized variance units, VRPt=(It/100)2−(365/30)Êtℙ[RVt,30]VRP_t=(I_t/100)^2-(365/30)\widehat E_t^{\mathbb P}[RV_{t,30}]. The uncertainty regression includes expected physical variance as a separate control.

Research design

Use a consistent broad-market variance proxy, estimate two physical forecasting procedures chronologically and relate their variance-premium proxies to lagged EPU and financial stress. Freeze the input vintage, release lags and common held-out dates.

Verify rights for EPU and other series. Keep a release-date ledger, include a predeclared crisis sensitivity, and report forecast uncertainty alongside association. A positive or negative association is not itself a causal finding or a return premium.

Required exhibits

Q/P variance and VRP proxy chart, forecast performance, EPU coefficient table and intervals, crisis and horizon sensitivity.

Minimum viable thesis

One verified 30-day proxy, two physical forecasts and conditional association to EPU on held-out dates, with clear units and uncertainty.

Scope and feasibility

Neither proxy is exact traded variance swap premium; physical forecasts are estimated and proxy market is not SPX. EPU measures news coverage, not structural uncertainty. No causality or return-premium equivalence. Source terms may restrict EPU and SPX redistribution.

Prerequisites: Options and volatility, forecasting, HAC and data handling.

Optional extension: Compare uncertainty association with realized stock-return forecasts using the same prequential VRP; preregister horizons.

Milestones

W1-3: definitions and rights; W4-6: matched labels; W7-9: forecast/VRP; W10-12: association and robustness; W13-16: report.
