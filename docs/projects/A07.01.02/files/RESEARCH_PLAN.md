# Research plan | A07.01.02

## Study design and independent work

Verify rights for EPU and other series. Keep a release-date ledger, include a predeclared crisis sensitivity, and report forecast uncertainty alongside association. A positive or negative association is not itself a causal finding or a return premium.

## Required tasks

1. Match VIX, market and 30-day future labels; document trading-day and annualization conventions.

2. Compare rolling variance and HAR-style forecasts with QLIKE and MSE on held-out dates.

3. Form VRP with prequential physical forecasts, estimate relation to lagged log EPU controlling for expected variance and lagged NFCI.

4. Contrast with ex-post VIX-squared minus realized variance and quantify forecast uncertainty via time blocks/refitting.

5. Examine crisis influence, alternate horizon proxies and common support; use HAC and paired calendar blocks.

## Required exhibits

Q/P variance and VRP proxy chart, forecast performance, EPU coefficient table and intervals, crisis and horizon sensitivity.

## Robustness

Neither proxy is exact traded variance swap premium; physical forecasts are estimated and proxy market is not SPX. EPU measures news coverage, not structural uncertainty. No causality or return-premium equivalence. Source terms may restrict EPU and SPX redistribution.

## Minimum viable thesis

One verified 30-day proxy, two physical forecasts and conditional association to EPU on held-out dates, with clear units and uncertainty.

## Optional extension

Compare uncertainty association with realized stock-return forecasts using the same prequential VRP; preregister horizons.

## Prerequisites

Options and volatility, forecasting, HAC and data handling.

## Thesis outline

1. Variance premium;

2. Q/P measurement;

3. Data and timing;

4. Forecasts and uncertainty relation;

5. Limitations.

## Milestones

W1-3: definitions and rights; W4-6: matched labels; W7-9: forecast/VRP; W10-12: association and robustness; W13-16: report.

At each meeting submit your independent code, parameter/input ledger, results and unresolved issues. Document failures and null findings.
