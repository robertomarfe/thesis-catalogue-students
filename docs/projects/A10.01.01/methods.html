Regularized Regressions for Equity Premium Forecasting

Do ridge, LASSO or elastic-net regressions improve one-month equity premium forecasts relative to the historical mean and ordinary least squares? Are improvements robust to tuning and evaluation windows?

Mathematical framework

For training-standardized zsz_s, minimize L(a,b)+αPη(b)L(a,b)+\alpha P_\eta(b), where

L(a,b)=12n∑s(ys+1−a−zs⊤b)2,L(a,b)=\frac{1}{2n}\sum_s(y_{s+1}-a-z_s^\top b)^2,

Pη(b)=(1−η)∥b∥22/2+η∥b∥1.P_\eta(b)=(1-\eta)\|b\|_2^2/2+\eta\|b\|_1. Ridge has η=0\eta=0, LASSO η=1\eta=1; elastic net is optional. Intercept is unpenalized. Choose penalties on matured chronological inner validation folds only, then refit on eligible training history; the final test block is for evaluation, not method selection.

LASSO commonly uses the first loss scaling. Record the actual estimator convention, not a shared numerical penalty across libraries.
