Goal

Covered interest parity links a currency forward to two funded cash accounts, but published monthly money-market proxies need not be synchronized tradable quotes. Construct parity-implied forward points and analyze their sensitivity, without interpreting the result as observed cross-currency basis.

Research question: How do the level and differential of short-term USD and EUR interest rates shape synthetic EUR/USD forward prices, and how sensitive is the result to rate conventions and proxy choice?

Student-owned research

Verify quote orientation, rate units, positive accrual, zero initial PV and reciprocal identity. Record availability and exact overlap. No observed basis or executable trade claim.

  1. Derive cash-and-carry with both quote orientations; demonstrate sign change when inverting rates/quote correctly.

  2. Build date-aligned rates and synthetic forwards, verify units, spot/forward points and parity on flat/zero differential tests.

  3. Decompose variation of log forward into log spot and log accrual ratio; report regime comparisons including negative-rate period.

  4. For representative contracts compute signed value after spot/rate shocks and sensitivity to day count and hypothetical cross-currency basis, using fixed contractual K. Distinguish remaining maturity from original tenor.

Minimum viable thesis: Verified spot and rate sources, correct parity and valuation identities, one tenor with historical synthetic points and stress/sensitivity analysis. Fallback to transparent synthetic scenarios if no overlapping rate series; empirical title/claim must then be reviewed.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
