D07.04.01 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

The HJM drift restriction ties forward-rate changes to discount-bond no-arbitrage. Finite grids can violate the continuous-time martingale identity numerically even when the model is correct. Distinguish this error from a genuinely misspecified zero-drift forward model.

Research question: How does the HJM drift restriction discipline the evolution of discount bond prices, and how large are discretization errors when a continuous-time no-arbitrage model is implemented on a finite grid?

Student-owned research

Keep the counterexample labeled inadmissible under money-market Q, not an alternative calibrated pricing model. Separate integration bias, sampling uncertainty and runtime.

  1. Derive bond dynamics by Ito and verify the drift cancellation.

  2. Implement exact Gaussian forward increments or fine-grid benchmark and compare Euler.

  3. Test E[P(t,T)/B(t)]=P(0,T) with paired MC uncertainty for each maturity and grid.

  4. Quantify omitted-drift distortion and compare time versus maturity integration errors.

  5. Reconstruct level, slope and curvature responses for one and two factors under matched initial curves.

Minimum viable thesis: One-factor deterministic volatility HJM, analytic drift, three maturity/grid checks and zero-drift counterexample; agreement with benchmark within numerical and MC tolerance.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
