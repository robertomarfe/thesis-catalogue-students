A12.01.02 · Asset Pricing · Portfolios & market frictions

Funding Constraints and the Performance of Low-Beta Strategies

Are US BAB returns lower when financial conditions tighten, and do lagged financial conditions predict subsequent BAB returns after controlling for standard factors?

Research overview

Motivation

Funding conditions may covary with betting-against-beta returns without establishing a structural borrowing-constraint channel.

Minimum viable thesis

Verified US BAB and NFCI monthly alignment, unconditional factor analysis, contemporaneous and lagged regressions with uncertainty, crisis and alternate proxy sensitivity, explicit noncausal interpretation. OOS prediction optional if sample too short.

Prerequisites

CAPM, multifactor regressions, funding liquidity economics, time-series HAC and Python.

Methods

Regression & inference · Time-series forecasting · Factor models & SDF
