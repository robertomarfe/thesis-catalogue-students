A06.03.02 · Asset Pricing · Consumption & equity valuation

Growth versus Value: Cash Flow Timing and Discount Rate Risk

How much greater is discount-rate sensitivity for stylized growth than value claims, and is that prediction consistent with descriptive portfolio evidence?

Synthetic core · Level 2/3 · Research draft

Synthetic core. No external dataset required for the core design; optional extensions may require data.

OverviewResearch proposalTheory & methodsLiteratureData & sourcesPython labResearch plan

Research overview

Motivation

Cash-flow timing produces a transparent difference in discount-rate exposure even when two claims have identical initial market value. An offsetting dividend revision separates a mechanical duration effect from a return response.

Minimum viable thesis

Two matched-price cash-flow profiles, analytic/numerical sensitivity and offsetting-news experiment. Historical evidence is optional S-class extension, explicitly distinguished from theory. Distinct from A06.01.01 valuation ratios and A06.02.01 strip data.

Prerequisites

Discounted cash flow, duration, numerical derivatives, regression for optional evidence.

Methods

Cash-flow valuation · Fixed income & curves · Equilibrium & preferences
