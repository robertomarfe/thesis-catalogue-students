Risk Parity versus Minimum-Variance Portfolios

Research question

Does equal risk contribution deliver more stable risk allocation than minimum variance, and at what cost in realized volatility and turnover?

Purpose. Understand the theoretical restrictions and plan an independent implementation. This note supplies derivations and conventions, not solved research results.

Mathematical framework

For variance v=w⊤Σwv=w^\top\Sigma w, normalized risk contribution is RCi=wi(Σw)i/vRC_i=w_i(\Sigma w)_i/v.

Contributions sum to one but can be negative for arbitrary portfolios. With positive budgets bib_i summing to one, solve the convex problem minx>0x⊤Σx/2−∑ibilogxi\min_{x>0}x^\top\Sigma x/2-\sum_i b_i\log x_i for SPD covariance, then normalize. FOC gives xi(Σx)i=bix_i(\Sigma x)_i=b_i. Diagonal covariance gives inverse-vol weights for equal budgets. Ex-ante budget equality is not realized risk equality.

Research checks

Check contribution sum, positive budget and ERC residual on diagonal and correlated SPD matrices; report KKT for GMV and eigenvalues. Compare four methods with common dates/covariance, cost drift and ex-ante contributions plus held-out realized contribution estimates and uncertainty.
