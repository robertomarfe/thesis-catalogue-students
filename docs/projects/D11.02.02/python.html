D11.02.02 | Student research starter

Current review draft; approval pending. Synthetic core; no mandatory external data.

Goal

Option prices can identify moments of the risk-neutral return distribution with sufficiently rich strike coverage. Empirical implementations truncate option strips and amplify noise in higher moments. The study quantifies this information loss relative to known synthetic truth and support-conditional bounds.

Research question: How accurately can the variance, skewness and kurtosis of risk-neutral log returns be recovered from truncated and discretely sampled option strips?

Student-owned research

Report absolute moment errors and cancellation across strip, tail and noise choices. Compare central moments only on admissible inputs. Record the exact support and quote tolerance for conditional bounds.

  1. Derive spanning formula and analytic mixture raw/central log moments.

  2. Recover moments from dense full-range strips; verify convergence to benchmark.

  3. Vary strike support, mesh and bid/ask noise; quantify bias and sampling variation separately.

  4. Construct two feasible nonnegative discrete Q distributions matching same finite quotes within tolerance but differing in fourth moment (LP on fixed support).

  5. Expand support and show tail assumption sensitivity; report undefined moments/negative estimated variance without forcing validity.

Minimum viable thesis: Analytic two-market truth, option-strip moment recovery, truncation/noise study and at least one support-conditional moment bound.

Record assumptions, independent checks, uncertainty and limitations in a separate research notebook. No core solver or research output is supplied.
