**Asset pricing · Portfolio choice, behavioral finance and market frictions · Level 2/3**

**Data:** Empirical design conditional on actual access and coverage. **Status:** current review draft; supervisor approval pending.

## Motivation

Sentiment may be associated with different subsequent anomaly returns. Prespecified published spreads test state-dependent residual performance without mining stock-level strategies.

## Research question

Do preselected equity anomaly portfolios earn different subsequent factor-adjusted returns in high versus low Baker-Wurgler sentiment states?

## Economic mechanism

Conditional alpha $\alpha_i(z)=a_i+c_i z$ in a factor regression that also lets beta depend on lagged sentiment state $z$.

## Research design

Baker–Wurgler sentiment, eight OSAP signals, FF3/5+Mom; expanding scaling, lagged states and familywise/FDR sensitivity on actual common coverage.

Pin actual sentiment workbook and matching eight OSAP signals/dictionary, state definition and fixed selection. Use lagged states and common months, show alpha=a+c*z and beta interactions, joint date-block uncertainty with familywise/FDR sensitivity and predeclared regime robustness. No data substitution without supervisor approval.

## Required exhibits

Signal selection log; conditional return plots; regression and joint inference table; lag/subsample sensitivity.

## Minimum viable thesis

Eight named portfolios, documented sentiment version, unconditional and conditional factor regressions, common-sample inference.

## Scope and feasibility

Do not use full-sample median for a trading rule or changes in revised sentiment as real-time innovations. Factor adjustment does not identify mispricing; account for correlated strategies, sample selection and revised data.

**Prerequisites:** Factor regressions, bootstrap, financial econometrics, Python.

**Optional extension:** Analyze cross-sectional heterogeneity by independently defined limits-to-arbitrage categories, not by selecting extreme estimated coefficients.
